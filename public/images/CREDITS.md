1. **Twemoji graphics** (most pictures). By Twitter, Inc. and other contributors; maintained fork: jdecked/twemoji, https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji. The README says "Graphics licensed under CC-BY 4.0" and that a mention in a README, About section, or website footer is acceptable attribution. License: **CC BY 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (see LICENSE-GRAPHICS in the repo: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/blob/main/LICENSE-GRAPHICS). Original SVG for each code: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jdecked/twemoji/main/assets/svg/<code>.svg` (cached in `_twemoji_src/`).
   **Changes made:** SVGs were rendered to PNG, resized, and combined with other emoji on a white card with a border. Some cards also have added arrows, lines, or text labels. These cards are **adaptations**, so CC BY 4.0 requires attribution + a link to the license + a note that changes were made (the attribution line below does this). 2. **Original drawings** (calendars, clock, timeline, phone frame, arrows, lines, text labels). These were drawn in code for this curriculum, with no outside source. The program may release them under the curriculum's own license (proposed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Section 7.4), or as CC0.

No photos or stock-site images are used.
