## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. Each Grammar page has one short Spanish note and one tip for speakers of other languages. Learners read them on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instructions:** use the same short task words every week: *Listen. Circle. Check. Write. Work with a partner. Work in a group. Ask a follow-up question.* Learners have the Task Words page from Lesson 1.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Do you write or speak? How many people do you talk to?"), **then go**. Say less, show more.

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Inside–outside circles:* the inner circle faces out, the outer circle faces in; the outer circle moves one person to the right each round. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Opening Talk + Homework Check · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, the same partner for both parts. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **I'm good at… / I'm interested in… / I don't mind…** + **-ing**
- **Opening Talk (6 min):** learners open the **Opening Talk** page. A asks a card question; B answers with 2–3 sentences; A asks one follow-up. Switch after each card.
- **Model with one learner:** "What are you good at?" → (answer) → "**Can you give me an example?**"
- **Homework Check (4 min):** partners read their Lesson 9 Homework item 2 (*Tell me about yourself*) to each other. Walk around. Write 2 common errors on the board (often *good at cook* or *interested on*) and correct them together.
- **Late arrivals:** join any pair as a group of 3.
- **If behind:** one card only, then the homework check.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:18 · New Words · 8 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the New Words page.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat once. Mime each action: **press** a button, **plug in** a cord, **turn on** a light, **turn** it **off**, put on **safety goggles**.
- **Show *step*:** count on your fingers: "Step 1, step 2, step 3."
- **Check meaning (1 min):** "You wash clothes with…?" (*detergent*) • "On a medicine bottle, you read the…?" (*label*) • "Before you log in, you type your…?" (*password*)
- **Pairs (2 min):** A says a word; B points to the picture and mimes it or makes a sentence (*Press START.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:18–6:30 · Listening + "Say it" · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Maria is a new worker at a supermarket. Her coworker Tom is teaching her to use a machine. What machine is it? Listen."
- **Play 1 → Task A** (what machine?). Pairs compare. Check together.
- **Read Tasks B and C together (1 min)** so learners know what to listen for.
- **Play 2 → Tasks B and C.** Pairs compare. Then show the answers (answer key).
- **"Say it" (2 min):** say: "For **th** in *then* and *the*, put your tongue between your teeth, and use your voice." Then *third*: the same tongue, no voice. Pause a little after each step. The class repeats each line in the box twice.
- **The audio plays the conversation once (about 1:11).** Press play again for play 2. A third play is fine if the class needs it.
- **If behind:** do Task C together on the board, reading the lines yourself.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:30–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 15 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.**
- **Show it (2 min):** make a cup of tea in mime and say each step: "**First**, boil the water. **Next**, put the tea bag in the cup. **Then**, add the water. **After that**, wait 3 minutes. **Finally**, drink it!" Hold up one finger for each step.
- **Boxes 1 and 2 (4 min):** read a question from the left; the class answers from the right. Point out: **no *you*** and **no *-s*** in an instruction (*Press START*).
- **Box 3 (2 min):** warnings: **Don't** + verb, **Don't forget to** + verb.
- **Box 4 (1 min):** the five sequence words, in order. Say them; the class repeats.
- **Check questions:** "___, put on your goggles. (step 1)" (*First*) • "___, turn it off. (the last step)" (*Finally*) • "Don't ___ (open) the door." (*open*) • "How ___ I log in?" (*do*)
- **Practice A and B (3 min):** learners write, then pairs check. Go over the answers.
- **Pair drill (last 2 min):** A asks "How do I make rice?" (or tea, a sandwich). B answers with 3 steps and sequence words. Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices it.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **First, … Next, … Then, … After that, … Finally, …** and **How do I…? / What do I do next? / Don't forget to…**
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · Talk Time: Teach Me in Four Steps · 12 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 trio. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. 5 or fewer: one learner teaches a strip to the group, and the group mimes.
- **How it works:** the Talk Time page has 4 strips. In each strip, the 4 pictures (A–D) are **not in order**. A chooses a strip, finds the right order (writes it under the strip), and teaches B with sequence words: *First, turn on the water. Next, use soap…* B mimes each step and asks *What do I do next?* Then switch with a new strip.
- **Model with one learner (1 min):** strip 1: "**First**, turn on the water." (point to B) "**Next**, use soap." (D) … The learner mimes.
- **Rounds (8 min):** about 2 minutes per strip; each pair does all 4 strips if there is time. Walk around and listen for the sequence words.
- **Finish (2 min):** ask 2 pairs to teach one strip to the class.
- **If behind:** 2 strips only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:17 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner writes **two sentences** on a slip: two steps for a task with sequence words (*First, open the app. Then, type your password.*), and one warning with *Don't* and a reason with *because* or *so* (*Don't open the door, because the water is hot.*). Collect at the door.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (one sentence on the board). Say: "About 15 minutes. Bring it next week."
:::

:::step Read & Write · 7:17–7:25 · A Medicine Label and 4 Steps · 8 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Say first:** "This label is invented. It's for reading practice only. For real medicine, always ask a pharmacist or a doctor."
- **Read (3 min):** learners read the label and answer questions 1–5. Say: "Read fast first. Then find the answers."
- **Pairs check (1 min):** compare answers; check together.
- **Write (4 min):** learners write 4 steps for a task they know (making coffee, washing a car, sending a text) with sequence words and one *Don't*.
- **If behind:** questions 1–5 only; the steps become homework.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 4 sentences in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle **a** or **b**.
- **Parts B and C:** learners do them alone. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "With a little help." 😐 "Not yet."
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 12).
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 12 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Listening script: `L3_L10_listening.mp3` (about 1:11; the conversation plays once, so press play again for the second listen)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer, at a normal, friendly speed.

> **Narrator:** Lesson ten. Listening. Maria is a new worker at a supermarket. Her coworker Tom is teaching her to use a machine. Listen.
> **Tom:** Hi, Maria. Welcome to the team! Today I'm going to show you the floor cleaning machine. It's easy.
> **Maria:** Great. How do I start?
> **Tom:** First, put on your safety goggles. Then, fill the tank with water.
> **Maria:** Why do I need goggles?
> **Tom:** Because the detergent can hurt your eyes. Next, add one cup of detergent. Not more!
> **Maria:** One cup. Got it. What do I do after that?
> **Tom:** After that, plug it in and turn it on. A green light comes on.
> **Maria:** And then I just push it?
> **Tom:** Right. Push it slowly. Don't leave it alone when it's on.
> **Maria:** What do I do when I finish?
> **Tom:** Finally, turn it off, unplug it, and empty the tank.
> **Maria:** Thanks, Tom. Can I try it now?
> **Tom:** Sure. Press the start button, and let's go. I'll walk with you.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Listening Task A:* **c** (a floor cleaning machine).
- *Listening Task B:* put on safety goggles **1** • fill the tank with water **2** • add detergent **3** • plug it in and turn it on **4** • turn it off and empty the tank **5** • How much detergent? **one cup** • The light is **green**.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 First, 2 Then, 3 Next, 4 After that, 5 Finally.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 First, 2 Next, 3 Then, 4 After that, 5 Finally.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 Press, 2 Don't open, 3 do, 4 to take, 5 Turn off.
- *Talk Time (the right order):* strip 1 (washing hands) **B, D, A, C** • strip 2 (coffee) **C, A, D, B** • strip 3 (a photo) **D, B, C, A** • strip 4 (parking) **A, D, B, C**. The sentences learners say will vary.
- *Homework 1:* 1 Press START. 2 Don't open the door. 3 Plug in the machine. / Plug the machine in. 4 Don't forget to take your card. *Homework 2, 3, and 4:* answers will vary.
- *Read & Write B:* 1 1 tablet • 2 every 8 hours • 3 with food • 4 3 tablets • 5 ask a pharmacist or a doctor • *Writing (C):* answers will vary.
- *Progress Check Part A (the teacher says, 2 times each):* 1 "First, plug it in. Then, turn it on." → **a** • 2 "Don't open the door." → **b** • 3 "After that, press START." → **a** • 4 "Finally, take your card." → **b**
- *Progress Check Part B:* 1 First, 2 Press, 3 Don't, 4 Finally.
- *Progress Check Part C:* answers will vary; for example 1 *First, open the app. Next, type your password. Then, press LOG IN.* 2 *Don't forget to take your receipt.*
- *Progress Check scoring:* A /4 + B /4 + C /2 = /10. This is not a test grade. It shows who needs more help hearing the steps (A) and with the forms (B, C).

### Teacher notes

- **The medicine label is invented (important):** it is for reading practice only. Tell learners to ask a pharmacist or a doctor about real medicine. Don't give medical advice, and don't ask learners about their own medicines.
- **Instructions have no *you* and no *-s*:** *Press START* (not *You press START* or *Presses START*). Negative: *Don't* + verb (*Don't open*).
- **Sequence words:** *first* (step 1), *next / then / after that* (the middle steps, in any order), *finally* (the last step). A comma after each one is fine but not important.
- ***Don't forget to…*** is a friendly reminder: *Don't forget to take your card.*
- **Review:** *You have to / You need to* also give instructions: *You need to plug it in first.* Accept it; don't teach it again.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *You press START*, *Presses START*, *No open the door*, *Don't forget take*, *Final, turn it off*. Say the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the Spanish note and the tip on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain them.
- **Mixed levels:** stronger learners add a *Don't…* warning to each strip. Newer learners use Boxes 1 and 4 as a script.

### Grammar Box 4: Sequence words

| STEP 1 | STEP 2 | STEP 3 | LAST STEP |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First**, … | **Next**, … | **Then**, … / **After that**, … | **Finally**, … |
| **First**, put on your goggles. | **Next**, fill the tank. | **Then**, plug it in. | **Finally**, turn it off. |

### Questions and answers

**Q: Do I say *you* in an instruction?**

A: **No.** Start with the verb: ***Press** START.* (not *You press START*)

**Q: *Press* or *Presses*?**

A: ***Press***. Instructions have no *-s*: ***Turn** it on. **Add** the soap.*

**Q: How do I say no in an instruction?**

A: ***Don't*** + verb: ***Don't open** the door.* (not *No open*)

**Q: *Don't forget take* or *Don't forget to take*?**

A: ***Don't forget to take** your card.*

**Q: *Finally* or *Final*?**

A: ***Finally***, for the last step: ***Finally**, turn it off.*

|---|---|
| You press START. | **Press** START. |

## Lesson 10 (Week 11) · Read & Write

### A. Read the medicine label. *(This label is invented. It's for reading practice only. For real medicine, ask a pharmacist or a doctor.)*

:::box OAK STREET PHARMACY · DAY COLD TABLETS · 24 tablets
**Directions:** Adults: take 1 tablet every 8 hours with food. Drink a full glass of water with each tablet. Do not take more than 3 tablets in 24 hours. Do not give to children under 12.
**Warnings:** This medicine can make you sleepy. Do not drive after you take it. If you feel worse after 3 days, stop and ask a pharmacist or a doctor.
**Store** at room temperature. Keep away from children.
