## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. Each Grammar page has one short Spanish note and one tip for speakers of other languages. Learners read them on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instructions:** use the same short task words every week: *Listen. Circle. Check. Write. Work with a partner. Work in a group. Ask a follow-up question.* Learners have the Task Words page from Lesson 1.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Do you write or speak? How many people do you talk to?"), **then go**. Say less, show more.

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Inside–outside circles:* the inner circle faces out, the outer circle faces in; the outer circle moves one person to the right each round. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

## Lesson 5 (Week 5): Don't Worry, I'll Do It

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- offer help with *I'll…* (*The phone's ringing. I'll get it.*)
- make a promise (*I'll call you back. Don't worry, I won't forget.*)
- ask and answer *Will you…? → Yes, I will. / No, I won't.*
- understand *Do you want me to…?* and *Shall I…?*
- choose *will* (decided now) or *going to* (planned before)
- read a school flyer and a sign-up sheet, and write a short text message offering help

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Opening Talk + Homework Check · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, the same partner for both parts. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **What were you doing at…? I was…ing when…**
- **Opening Talk (6 min):** learners open the **Opening Talk** page. A asks a card question; B answers with 2–3 sentences; A asks one follow-up. Switch after each card.
- **Model with one learner:** "What were you doing at 8:00 last night?" → (answer) → "**Really? Who were you with?**"
- **Homework Check (4 min):** partners read their Lesson 4 Homework item 1 (*At 7:00 a.m., I was…*) to each other. Walk around. Write 2 common errors on the board (often *I was cook* or *They was…*) and correct them together.
- **Late arrivals:** join any pair as a group of 3.
- **If behind:** one card only, then the homework check.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:18 · New Words · 8 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the New Words page.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat once. Act out **carry** (pick up a bag), **offer** (raise your hand: "I'll help!"), **promise** (hand on your heart: "I promise"), and **clean up** (sweep).
- **Show *offer*:** pick up a heavy bag and look tired. Wait. When a learner says something, smile: "Thank you! That's an **offer**." Then say it for them: "**I'll carry it.**"
- **Check meaning (1 min):** "You give your pen to a classmate for 5 minutes. Is that **lend** or **give**?" (*lend*) • "You drive your neighbor to work. You…?" (*give a ride*) • "Before a party, you put out the chairs. You…?" (*set up*) • "After the party?" (*clean up*)
- **Pairs (2 min):** A says a word; B points to the picture and makes a sentence (*I can give you a ride.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:18–6:30 · Listening + "Say it" · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** point to the **school event** picture on the New Words page. Say: "Carmen and David are parents. Their children go to Hillside School. Carmen is calling David. What is the event? Listen."
- **Play 1 → Task A** (what is the event?). Pairs compare. Check together.
- **Read Tasks B and C together (1 min)** so learners know what to listen for. For Task B, show the letters: **D** = David, **R** = Rosa, **S** = David's sister, **C** = Carmen.
- **Play 2 → Tasks B and C.** Pairs compare. Then show the answers (answer key).
- **"Say it" (2 min):** say: "*I will* is usually ***I'll***: one short sound." Read *I'll, you'll, we'll*. Then contrast ***won't*** (like *don't*) and ***want***: *I won't forget. / I want pizza.* The class repeats each line twice.
- **The audio plays the conversation once (about 1:15).** Press play again for play 2. A third play is fine if the class needs it.
- **If behind:** do Task C together on the board, reading the lines yourself.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:30–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 15 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the Grammar page. The **left side is the problem or the question. The right side is the answer.**
- **Box 1 (3 min):** act it out. Drop a pen: "Oh no!" A learner picks it up: "**I'll get it.**" Read Box 1: you read the problem; the class answers with *I'll…*.
- **Box 2 (3 min):** promises. Ask a learner: "**Will you** bring your homework next week?" → "Yes, I will." → "Promise?" → "**I promise.** I won't forget." Point to **won't = will not**.
- **Box 3 (1 min):** *Do you want me to…? / Shall I…?* are other ways to offer. Learners only need to understand them and answer *Yes, please. / No, thanks.*
- **Box 4 (3 min):** draw two pictures on the board: a **light bulb** (an idea **now** → *I'll*) and a **calendar** (planned **before** → *I'm going to*). Read Box 4 across.
- **Check questions:** "The phone's ringing. I ___ get it." (*'ll*) • "I bought a ticket. I ___ visit my mom." (*'m going to*) • "Don't worry, I ___ forget." (*won't*)
- **Practice A and B (3 min):** learners write, then pairs check. Go over the answers.
- **Pair drill (last 2 min):** A says a problem (*I'm hungry. / It's cold. / My bag is heavy.*); B offers help with *I'll…*. Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices it.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **I'll… / Don't worry, I won't… / Thanks! That's very kind.** and **I'm going to… (I planned it.)**
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · Talk Time: Problem / Offer Cards + Event Board · 12 min
- **Grouping:** Round 1 pairs; Round 2 groups of 3–4. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 trio, then 4 groups. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs, then 2 groups. 5 or fewer: one group for both rounds.
- **Round 1, pairs (5 min):** A reads a problem card (*My car won't start.*). B offers help with *I'll…* and one reason or detail (*I'll give you a ride, because I'm going that way.*). A says thank you (*Thanks! That's very kind.*). Switch after each card. Try to use all 8 cards.
- **Model with one learner (1 min):** read card 1. Help the learner say "*I'll give you a ride.*" Answer: "**Really? Thank you so much!**"
- **Round 2, groups (6 min):** each group chooses one event on the Event Board: **a park cleanup** or **a school family night**. Each person volunteers for **two jobs** with *I'll…* and writes their name. Then each person says **one plan** with *going to* (something they planned before): *I'm going to work on Saturday morning, so I'll come at 11:00.*
- **Share (1 min):** one person from each group reads their board: "*Ana will bring water. Tom will set up the tables.*"
- **If behind:** Round 1 only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:17 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner writes **two sentences** on a slip: one offer or promise with *I'll* or *I won't*, and one plan with *going to* and *because* or *so* (*I'm going to visit my sister on Sunday, because it's her birthday.*). Collect at the door.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (one offer on the board). Say: "About 15 minutes. Bring it next week."
:::

:::step Read & Write · 7:17–7:25 · A Flyer, a Sign-Up Sheet, and a Text Message · 8 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Read (3 min):** learners read the flyer and the volunteer sign-up sheet and answer questions 1–5. Say: "Read fast first. Then find the answers."
- **Pairs check (1 min):** compare answers; check together.
- **Write (4 min):** learners choose one open job on the sign-up sheet and write a short text message to Carmen offering help, with *I'll* (and *going to* if they want). Show the example.
- **If behind:** questions 1–5 only; the text message becomes homework.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 4 sentences in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle **a** (decided now) or **b** (planned before).
- **Parts B and C:** learners do them alone. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "With a little help." 😐 "Not yet."
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 6).
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 6 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Teacher notes

- **Invented events (important):** every event, job, and sign-up sheet in this lesson is invented. Volunteering is an example, not a request: never ask learners to sign up for a real event, give a real phone number, or offer a real ride. "I can't help this time" is always an OK answer.
- ***will* never changes:** *I will, she will, they will*. No *-s*, no *to*: *She'll call* (not *She wills call*, not *She will to call*).
- ***will* or *going to*:** *I'll* = I decide **now** (an offer, a promise, a quick decision). *going to* = I **planned it before** (I bought the ticket; I ordered the pizza). Don't spend a long time on cases where both are possible; in real life, both are often fine.
- ***Shall I…? / Do you want me to…?*** are for understanding only this week. Learners answer *Yes, please. / No, thanks, I'm OK.*
- **Mixed levels:** stronger learners add a reason to each offer (*I'll drive, because I have a big car.*). Newer learners use Box 1 as a script in Talk Time.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *I help you* (for an offer), *I will to bring*, *She wills*, *Don't worry, I don't forget*, *I'll go to the dentist tomorrow* (when it's already planned). Say the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the Spanish note and the tip on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain them.

### Questions and answers

**Q: Does *will* change for *he* or *she*?**

A: **No.** *I will, he will, she will, they will.* Then use the base verb: *She'll **call*** (not *wills*, not *will to call*). *I'll* = *I will*; *won't* = *will not*.

**Q: When do I use *will*?**

A: For something you **decide now**: an offer (*I'll help you*), a promise (*I'll call you back*), or a quick decision (*I'll have the soup*).

**Q: When do I use *going to*?**

A: For a **plan you made before**: *I'm going to visit my sister on Sunday.* (I bought the ticket last week.)

**Q: How do I say it?**

A: *I'll* is one short sound (like *aisle*). *won't* sounds like *don't*. *want* is different: *I **want** pizza.*

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| The phone's ringing. I answer it. | The phone's ringing. **I'll** answer it. |
| I will to bring the drinks. | I **will bring** (I'll bring) the drinks. |
| She wills call you. | She **will** call you. |
| Don't worry, I don't forget. | Don't worry, I **won't** forget. |
| Will you calling me? | Will you **call** me? |
| I'll visit my sister Sunday. (I bought the ticket.) | I'**m going to** visit my sister on Sunday. |

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** en español muchas veces usamos el presente para ofrecer ayuda: *¿Te ayudo? Yo lo traigo.* En inglés se necesita *will*: *I'll help you. I'll bring it.* (no *I help you*, no *I bring it*). *won't* = *will not*: *I won't forget* = *No me voy a olvidar.*

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** *will* never changes: *I will, she will, they will*. No *-s* and no *to* after it.
