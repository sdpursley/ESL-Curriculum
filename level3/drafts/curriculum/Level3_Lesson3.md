## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. Each Grammar page has one short Spanish note and one tip for speakers of other languages. Learners read them on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instructions:** use the same short task words every week: *Listen. Circle. Check. Write. Work with a partner. Work in a group. Ask a follow-up question.* Learners have the Task Words page from Lesson 1.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Do you write or speak? How many people do you talk to?"), **then go**. Say less, show more.

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Inside–outside circles:* the inner circle faces out, the outer circle faces in; the outer circle moves one person to the right each round. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

## Lesson 3 (Week 3): When I Was Young

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- say what they used to do (*I used to help on the farm.*)
- say what they didn't use to do (*I didn't use to drink coffee.*)
- ask and answer *Did you use to…? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.*
- compare then and now (*I used to write letters. Now I send text messages.*)
- tell the difference between a past habit (*used to*), one time in the past (simple past), and now (simple present)
- read a short memory story and write 4 sentences about then and now
:::

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Opening Talk + Homework Check · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, the same partner for both parts. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **What does he / she look like? What's he / she like?**
- **Opening Talk (6 min):** learners open the **Opening Talk** page. A asks a card question; B answers with 2–3 sentences; A asks one follow-up. Switch after each card.
- **Model with one learner:** "Tell me about your best friend. What's she like?" → (answer) → "**Really? What does she look like?**"
- **Homework Check (4 min):** partners read their Lesson 2 Homework item 1 (the person they described) to each other. Walk around. Write 2 common errors on the board (often *long hairs* or *What does she like?*) and correct them together.
- **Late arrivals:** join any pair as a group of 3.
- **If behind:** one card only, then the homework check.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:18 · New Words · 8 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the New Words page.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat once. Act out **climb trees** (climb with your arms), **jump rope** (turn a rope and jump), and **feed the animals** (throw corn to chickens).
- **Then / now:** point to the last picture. Say: "**Then**, people listened to the radio. **Now**, we use a phone." Make two columns on the board: **THEN** and **NOW**. Learners say where *radio, letter, landline phone, text message* go.
- **Check meaning (1 min):** "Is a **village** big or small?" (small) • "Are **chores** fun or work?" (work: cleaning, cooking, washing) • "Is your **childhood** now or in the past?" (in the past)
- **Pairs (2 min):** A says a word; B points to the picture and makes a sentence (*My grandmother had a farm.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:18–6:30 · Listening + "Say it" · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** point to the old photo on the Listening page. Say: "This is Daniel. This is his mother, Teresa. They're looking at an old photo of Teresa's childhood. Where did she live? Listen."
- **Play 1 → Task A** (then or now). Pairs compare. Check together.
- **Read Tasks B and C together (1 min)** so learners know what to listen for.
- **Play 2 → Tasks B and C.** Pairs compare. Then show the answers (answer key).
- **"Say it" (2 min):** say: "*used to* sounds like **yoos-tuh**." Read the line in the "Say it" box, then *Did you use to walk?* and *I didn't use to have a TV.* The class repeats each line twice. Point out that *used to* and *use to* sound the same.
- **The audio plays the conversation once (about 1:20).** Press play again for play 2. A third play is fine if the class needs it.
- **If behind:** do Task C together on the board, reading the lines yourself.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:30–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 15 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.**
- **Draw a timeline on the board:** a long line with **THEN** on the left and **NOW** on the right. Draw many small marks on the THEN side and write: **I used to walk to school (many times).** On the NOW side write: **Now I drive.**
- **Box 1 (3 min):** read a question from the left; the class answers from the right. Then ask learners: "What did you use to do after school?"
- **Box 2 (3 min):** questions and short answers: *Did you use to…? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.* Point to **use** (no *-d*) after *did* and *didn't*.
- **Boxes 3 and 4 (3 min):** read the THEN / NOW rows. Then read Box 4: a habit (*used to*), one time (*went*), now (*usually*).
- **Check questions:** "I ___ live in a village." (*used to*) • "Did you ___ walk to school?" (*use to*) • "Last Saturday I ___ to the park." (*went*, one time) • "Now I ___ drive to work." (*usually*)
- **Practice A and B (3 min):** learners write, then pairs check. Go over the answers.
- **Pair drill (last 2 min):** A asks *Did you use to…?* with a word from the New Words page; B answers with a short answer and one more sentence. Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices it.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **Did you use to…? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.** and **Where? Why? When? What about now?**
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · Talk Time: Then and Now Mingle · 12 min
- **Grouping:** mingle, then groups of 3. 15 learners: everyone stands. 7–8 learners: everyone stands. 5 or fewer: sit together and ask everyone.
- **Round 1, mingle (7 min):** learners take the "Then and Now" sheet. They walk and ask one classmate one question (*Did you use to have a radio?*). If the answer is **Yes**, they write the name and ask one follow-up (*Where? Why?*), and write a short answer. If **No**, they say thank you and ask another person. Try to get a different name for each line.
- **Model with one learner (1 min):** "Did you use to walk far to school?" → "Yes, I did." → "**Really? How far?**" → "About one hour." Write the name and "1 hour."
- **Round 2, groups of 3 (4 min):** learners fill in 3 rows of the "My Then and Now Chart" (1 minute). Then each learner says **3 sentences** comparing then and now (*I used to live in a village. Now I live in a city. I didn't use to cook, but now I cook every day.*). Listeners ask one follow-up each.
- **Baseline snapshot:** while learners talk, finish the Lesson 1 baseline checklist with the last learners.
- **If behind:** the mingle only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:17 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner writes **two sentences** on a slip: one with *used to* or *didn't use to*, and one about now with *because* or *so* (*I used to walk to work. Now I take the bus because I live far away.*). Collect at the door.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (one *used to* sentence on the board). Say: "About 15 minutes. Bring it next week."
:::

:::step Read & Write · 7:17–7:25 · A Memory Story · 8 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Read (3 min):** learners read the story on the Read & Write page and answer questions 1–5. Say: "Read fast first. Then find the answers."
- **Pairs check (2 min):** compare answers; check together.
- **Write (3 min):** learners write 4 sentences: 2 with *used to* (then) and 2 about now. Any place they used to live, or any job they used to have, is OK.
- **If behind:** questions 1–5 only; the writing becomes homework.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 4 sentences in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners check **Then** or **Now**.
- **Parts B and C:** learners do them alone. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "With a little help." 😐 "Not yet."
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 4).
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 4 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Teacher notes

- **Choice and comfort (important):** learners choose what to share. Childhood can be hard for some people. "A place I used to live," "a job I used to have," or an invented story are always OK. Never ask why someone moved or left a country. "I don't want to say" is always OK.
- **What *used to* means:** something that happened **many times** in the past and **is not true now**. If it happened one time, use the simple past (*Last year I went to Texas*).
- ***used to* vs. *use to*:** after *did* and *didn't*, write **use** (no *-d*): *Did you use to…? I didn't use to…* They sound the same, so this is a spelling point. Don't spend a lot of time on it.
- **Now:** for now, use the simple present with *usually / every day*: *Now I usually drive.* Learners sometimes say *I use to drive* for now; say "*Now* you **usually** drive."
- **Mixed levels:** stronger learners add a reason to each then/now sentence (*I used to walk, because we didn't have a car*). Newer learners use Box 1 and Box 3 as a script in Talk Time.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *I use to live…* (for the past), *Did you used to…?*, *I didn't used to…*, *I used to go to Texas last year* (one time), *I am used to play soccer*. Say the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the Spanish note and the tip on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain them.

## Lesson 3 (Week 3) · Grammar

**Read across. A reads from the left side. B answers from the right side.** Choose one part from each column.

### Grammar Box 1: *What did you use to do?*

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | < | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| What **did** | you | **use to** do | after school? / on weekends? | ‖ | I **used to** | help on the farm. / play soccer. / do chores. |
| What **did** | she / he | **use to** do | in the evening? / in the summer? | ‖ | She **used to** / He **used to** | listen to the radio. / swim in the river. |
| Where **did** | you | **use to** | live? / work? | ‖ | I **used to** | live in a village. / work on a farm. |

### Grammar Box 2: *Did you use to…?* Short answers

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | < | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Did** | you | **use to** | walk to school? / climb trees? | ‖ | Yes, | I | **did**. |
| ^ | ^ | ^ | ^ | ‖ | No, | I | **didn't**. |
| **Did** | your family | **use to** | have a radio? / have a TV? | ‖ | Yes, | we | **did**. |
| ^ | ^ | ^ | ^ | ‖ | No, | we | **didn't**. |

<!-- subst -->
| THEN (*used to / didn't use to*) | ‖ | NOW (simple present) |
|---|---|---|
| I **used to** write letters. | ‖ | Now I **send** text messages. |
| I **didn't use to** drink coffee. | ‖ | Now I **drink** it every day. |
| We **used to** live in a village. | ‖ | Now we **live** in a city. |
| She **used to** be very shy. | ‖ | Now she's **very friendly**. |

### Grammar Box 4: A habit, one time, or now?

<!-- subst -->
| A HABIT IN THE PAST (many times) | ‖ | ONE TIME IN THE PAST | ‖ | NOW |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I **used to** walk to school. | ‖ | One day I **took** the bus. | ‖ | Now I usually **drive**. |
| We **used to** eat dinner at 8:00. | ‖ | Last night we **ate** at 6:00. | ‖ | Now we usually **eat** at 7:00. |

### Questions and answers

**Q: What does *used to* mean?**

A: Something happened **many times** in the past, and it's **not true now**. *I **used to** live on a farm* (but now I live in a city).

**Q: Does *used to* change for *he* or *she*?**

A: **No.** *I used to, she used to, they used to.* Then use the base verb: *She used to **walk*** (not *walked*).

**Q: When do I write *use to* (no *-d*)?**

A: After ***did*** and ***didn't***: *Did you **use to** walk? I didn't **use to** drink coffee.*

**Q: Can I use *used to* for one time?**

A: **No.** For one time, use the simple past: *Last summer I **went** to the beach.* (not *I used to go*)

**Q: Can I use *used to* for now?**

A: **No.** *used to* is only for the past. For now, say ***usually*** or ***every day***: *Now I **usually** take the bus.*

**Q: How do I say it?**

A: *used to* and *use to* both sound like **"yoos-tuh."**

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| I use to live on a farm. (the past) | I **used to** live on a farm. |
| Did you used to walk to school? | Did you **use to** walk to school? |
| I didn't used to drink coffee. | I didn't **use to** drink coffee. |
| She used to walked to school. | She used to **walk** to school. |
| I used to go to Texas last year. | I **went** to Texas last year. |
| I use to take the bus now. | **Now I usually** take the bus. |

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** *used to* es como el imperfecto (-aba / -ía) o *solía*: *I used to play* = Jugaba / Solía jugar. Úsalo solo para el pasado. En preguntas y negativos se escribe *use* (sin *-d*): *Did you use to…? I didn't use to…* Para una sola vez usa el pasado simple: *Ayer fui* = *Yesterday I went* (no *used to go*).

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** *used to* is only for the past. For now, say *I usually…*. For one time in the past, use the simple past (*I went, I saw*).
