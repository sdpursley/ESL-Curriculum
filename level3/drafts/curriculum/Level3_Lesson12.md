## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. Each Grammar page has one short Spanish note and one tip for speakers of other languages. Learners read them on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instructions:** use the same short task words every week: *Listen. Circle. Check. Write. Work with a partner. Work in a group. Ask a follow-up question.* Learners have the Task Words page from Lesson 1.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Do you write or speak? How many people do you talk to?"), **then go**. Say less, show more.

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Inside–outside circles:* the inner circle faces out, the outer circle faces in; the outer circle moves one person to the right each round. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

## Lesson 12 (Week 13): Something's Wrong

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- say what's wrong in a home: *Something is wrong with the heater. The sink is leaking.*
- use *something / anything / nothing* and *someone / anyone / no one*
- ask for a repair and agree on a day and time: *Can you send someone? Is anyone home on Tuesday?*
- use one negative only: *I didn't see anything.* (not *I didn't see nothing*)
- read a maintenance request and write a short one (3–5 sentences)
:::

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Opening Talk + Homework Check · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, the same partner for both parts. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **How was it? It was… -ing / I was… -ed**
- **Opening Talk (6 min):** learners open the **Opening Talk** page. A asks a card question; B answers with 2–3 sentences; A asks one follow-up. Switch after each card.
- **Model with one learner:** "What was the last movie you saw? How was it?" → (answer) → "**How did you feel at the end?**"
- **Homework Check (4 min):** partners read their Lesson 11 Homework item 2 to each other. Walk around. Write 2 common errors on the board (often *I was boring* or *It was excited*) and correct them together.
- **Late arrivals:** join any pair as a group of 3.
- **If behind:** one card only, then the homework check.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:18 · New Words · 8 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the New Words page.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat once. Mime: water drops from a **leak**, try a door with a broken **lock**, cover your ears for a **smoke alarm**: "Beep! Beep!"
- **Show *landlord* and *repair person*:** "The **landlord** owns the building. The **repair person** fixes things."
- **Check meaning (1 min):** "Water is coming from the pipe. It's a…?" (*leak*) • "The room is cold. What's wrong?" (*the heater*) • "You write about a problem on a…?" (*maintenance request*)
- **Pairs (2 min):** A says a word; B points to the picture and makes a sentence (*The sink is leaking.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:18–6:30 · Listening + "Say it" · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Sara lives in an apartment. She's calling the building office. What problems does she have? Listen."
- **Play 1 → Task A** (which problems?). Pairs compare. Check together.
- **Read Tasks B and C together (1 min)** so learners know what to listen for.
- **Play 2 → Tasks B and C.** Pairs compare. Then show the answers (answer key).
- **"Say it" (2 min):** say: "Stress the first part: **SOME**thing, **AN**yone, **NO** one." Then *thing*: tongue between the teeth, **th** (not *ting*). The class repeats each word twice, then the line in the box.
- **The audio plays the conversation once (about 1:15).** Press play again for play 2. A third play is fine if the class needs it.
- **If behind:** do Task C together on the board, reading the lines yourself.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:30–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 15 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.**
- **Show it (2 min):** look into an empty cup: "Is there **anything** in it? No, there isn't **anything**. There's **nothing** in it." Look at the door: "Is **anyone** at the door? No, **no one** is there."
- **Boxes 1, 2, and 3 (5 min):** read a question from the left; the class answers from the right.
- **Box 4 (2 min):** the table: **some-** in + sentences, **any-** in questions and with *not*, **no-** alone. Point to the one-negative rule: *I didn't see anything* = *I saw nothing*.
- **Check questions:** "Is ___ wrong?" (*anything*) • "Yes, ___ is wrong with the sink." (*something*) • "No, ___ is home." (*no one*) • "I didn't hear ___." (*anything*)
- **Practice A and B (3 min):** learners write, then pairs check. Go over the answers.
- **Pair drill (last 2 min):** A points to a picture on the New Words page: "Is anything wrong with the ___?" B answers: *Yes, something is wrong with it. It's…* or *No, nothing is wrong.* Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices it.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **Is anything wrong? / Something is wrong with… / Is anyone home? / No one is home. / Can you send someone?** Turn pairs of chairs back to back for Round 1.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · Talk Time: The Repair Call · 12 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, back to back (like a phone call: no looking!). 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 trio (two tenants). 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. 5 or fewer: one manager, the others call one by one.
- **Round 1 (7 min):** A is a **tenant** with a Tenant Card; B is the **building manager** with the Repair Schedule. A calls: says what's wrong and since when. B asks one question (*Is there any water on the floor?*) and finds an **open** time when someone is home. They agree on a day and time; B writes it in the schedule. Switch roles with a new card.
- **Model with one learner (1 min):** you are the tenant with card 1: "Hi, this is apartment 3A. Something is wrong with my toilet. It's leaking." The manager: "Is anyone home on Monday at 4:00?" → "Yes, someone is home after 3."
- **Round 2, Find Something (4 min):** pairs look at the apartment picture and find **7 problems**. They say *There's something wrong with the… / There's a… on the… / There isn't anything wrong with the chair.* The first pair to find all 7 wins.
- **If behind:** Round 1 only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:17 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner writes **two sentences** on a slip: one with *something / anything / nothing* or *someone / anyone / no one* (*Something is wrong with my heater.*), and one with *if* or *so* (*No one is home in the morning, so please come at 3.*). Collect at the door.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (one sentence on the board). Say: "About 15 minutes. Bring it next week."
:::

:::step Read & Write · 7:17–7:25 · A Maintenance Request · 8 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Read (3 min):** learners read the request form and Ken's note and answer questions 1–5. Say: "Read fast first. Then find the answers."
- **Pairs check (1 min):** compare answers; check together.
- **Write (4 min):** learners write a 3–5 sentence maintenance request: the problem, since when, the best time, and a phone number. Say: "Invent the apartment, the problem, and a 555 number."
- **If behind:** questions 1–5 only; the request becomes homework.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 4 sentences in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle **a** or **b**.
- **Parts B and C:** learners do them alone. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "With a little help." 😐 "Not yet."
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 14).
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 14 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Listening script: `L3_L12_listening.mp3` (about 1:15; the conversation plays once, so press play again for the second listen)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer, at a normal, friendly speed.

> **Narrator:** Lesson twelve. Listening. Sara lives in an apartment. She's calling the building office about some problems. Listen.
> **Mr. Grant:** Maple Court Apartments, this is Mr. Grant. How can I help you?
> **Sara:** Hi, this is Sara in apartment 4B. Something is wrong with my kitchen sink. It's leaking.
> **Mr. Grant:** Oh no. Is there any water on the floor?
> **Sara:** Yes, a little. I put a towel under it. And there's something else. The heater doesn't work.
> **Mr. Grant:** Do you see anything on the screen?
> **Sara:** No, I don't see anything. The screen is black, and the apartment is really cold.
> **Mr. Grant:** OK. I'll send someone tomorrow morning. Is anyone home at nine?
> **Sara:** No, no one is home in the morning. I work until two.
> **Mr. Grant:** How about Tuesday at three?
> **Sara:** Tuesday at three is fine. If it gets worse tonight, I'll call you.
> **Mr. Grant:** Great. The repair person will fix both things. Until then, don't use the sink.
> **Sara:** OK, I won't. Thank you so much.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Listening Task A:* ✓ **the kitchen sink** (it's leaking) and ✓ **the heater** (it doesn't work). ✗ the toilet, ✗ the lock, ✗ a mouse.
- *Listening Task B:* 1 **4B** • 2 **a little** water • 3 **black** • 4 **Tuesday** • 5 **3:00**.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 Something, 2 any, 3 something, 4 anything, 5 someone, 6 anyone, 7 no one.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 something, 2 anything, 3 nothing, 4 someone, 5 anyone, 6 No one.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 anything, 2 someone, 3 anything, 4 nothing, 5 anyone.
- *Talk Time Round 1 (possible open times):* card 1 → Mon 4:00 or Tue 4:00 • card 2 → Tue 9:00 or Fri 1:00 • card 3 → Thu 9:00 or Thu 1:00 • card 4 → Mon 1:00, Mon 4:00, or Fri 1:00. The conversations will vary.
- *Talk Time Round 2 (7 problems):* the smoke alarm is beeping • the light bulb is out (off) • the sink is leaking • the heater doesn't work (it's cold) • the door lock is open / broken • a cockroach on the wall • a mouse on the floor. (Nothing is wrong with the plant, the chair, or the picture.)
- *Homework 1:* 1 Something is wrong with the heater. 2 Is anyone home? 3 I didn't see anything. / I saw nothing. 4 No one called. *Homework 2, 3, and 4:* answers will vary.
- *Read & Write B:* 1 apartment 2C • 2 the bathroom light (it doesn't turn on) and the lock on the front door (it's hard to close) • 3 since last Friday • 4 after 5:00 p.m. on weekdays, or Saturday • 5 call or text Ken at 555-0191 • *Writing (C):* answers will vary.
- *Progress Check Part A (the teacher says, 2 times each):* 1 "Something is wrong with the sink." → **a** • 2 "No one is home on Monday." → **b** • 3 "I didn't see anything." → **b** • 4 "I'll send someone on Friday." → **a**
- *Progress Check Part B:* 1 anything, 2 Someone, 3 anything, 4 no one.
- *Progress Check Part C:* answers will vary; for example 1 *Something is wrong with my heater. It doesn't work.* 2 *Can you send someone on Tuesday after 3:00?*
- *Progress Check scoring:* A /4 + B /4 + C /2 = /10. This is not a test grade. It shows who needs more help hearing *some- / any- / no-* (A) and with the forms (B, C).

### Teacher notes

- **Keep it practical and invented (important):** never ask about learners' real rent, landlord, or living situation. Use the cards and invented apartments. Tenant rights are a Level 5 topic; if a learner asks, point to the local resources on the "Keep practicing" sheet.
- **The rule in one line:** *some-* in + sentences (*Something is wrong*), *any-* in questions and with *not* (*Is anything wrong? I didn't see anything*), *no-* alone (*Nothing is wrong. No one is home*).
- **One negative only:** *I didn't see anything* or *I saw nothing* (not *I didn't see nothing*).
- ***Something is wrong with…*** is the most useful sentence today. Practice it with many nouns.
- **Review:** *If it gets worse, I'll call you* (Lesson 6) and *I'll send someone* (Lesson 5). Accept both; don't teach them again.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *I didn't see nothing*, *Is something wrong?* (OK in real life, but teach *anything* first), *Nobody is not home*, *Anything is wrong* (in a + sentence), *some one* as two words. Say the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the Spanish note and the tip on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain them.
- **Mixed levels:** stronger learners add *since…* and an *If…, I'll…* sentence to each call. Newer learners use Boxes 1 and 2 as a script.

### Grammar Box 4: *some- / any- / no-*

| | THINGS | PEOPLE | EXAMPLE |
|---|---|---|---|
| + sentence | **something** | **someone** | *Something is wrong.* |
| question | **anything** | **anyone** | *Is anyone home?* |
| with *not* | not… **anything** | not… **anyone** | *I didn't see anything.* |
| alone | **nothing** | **no one** | *No one is home.* |

### Questions and answers

**Q: *I didn't see nothing*?**

A: **No.** One negative only: *I **didn't** see **anything**.* or *I saw **nothing**.*

**Q: *something* or *anything*?**

A: ***something*** in + sentences: *Something is wrong.* ***anything*** in questions and with *not*: *Is anything wrong?*

**Q: *no one* or *nobody*?**

A: The same meaning: *No one is home. / Nobody is home.*

**Q: How do I start a repair call?**

A: *Hi, this is… in apartment… **Something is wrong with** my…*

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| I didn't see nothing. | I didn't see **anything**. |
| Anything is wrong with the sink. | **Something** is wrong with the sink. |
| No one is not home. | **No one** is home. |
| Is some one home? | Is **anyone** home? |

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** en español se usan dos negativos (*No veo nada*). En inglés, solo uno: *I don't see anything* o *I see nothing* (no *I don't see nothing*).

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** in questions, *anything* and *anyone* are the safe choice: *Is anything wrong?*
