## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. Each Grammar page has one short Spanish note and one tip for speakers of other languages. Learners read them on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instructions:** use the same short task words every week: *Listen. Circle. Check. Write. Work with a partner. Work in a group. Ask a follow-up question.* Learners have the Task Words page from Lesson 1.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Do you write or speak? How many people do you talk to?"), **then go**. Say less, show more.

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Inside–outside circles:* the inner circle faces out, the outer circle faces in; the outer circle moves one person to the right each round. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

## Lesson 11 (Week 12): Bored or Boring?

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- say how something was: *It was exciting. / It was boring.*
- say how they felt: *I was excited. / I was bored.*

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Opening Talk + Homework Check · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, the same partner for both parts. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **First, … Next, … Then, … Finally, …**
- **Opening Talk (6 min):** learners open the **Opening Talk** page. A asks a card question; B answers with 2–3 sentences; A asks one follow-up. Switch after each card.
- **Model with one learner:** "How do you make your favorite food?" → (answer) → "**What do you do after that?**"
- **Homework Check (4 min):** partners read their Lesson 10 Homework item 2 (4 steps) to each other. The partner mimes the steps. Walk around. Write 2 common errors on the board (often *You press* or *No open*) and correct them together.
- **Late arrivals:** join any pair as a group of 3.
- **If behind:** one card only, then the homework check.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:18 · New Words · 8 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the New Words page.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say both words. Learners repeat once. For each pair, point to the left picture: "**It** was exciting." Point to the face: "**I** was excited."
- **Show it with your face:** yawn: "The movie was **boring**. I was **bored**." Look confused: "The map was **confusing**. I was **confused**."
- **Check meaning (1 min):** "A big event in a park with food and music is a…?" (*festival*) • "You see old pictures and things at a…?" (*museum*) • "You go to hear music at a…?" (*concert*)
- **Pairs (2 min):** A points to a picture; B says two sentences (*It was relaxing. I was relaxed.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:18–6:30 · Listening + "Say it" · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "It's Monday. Elena and Luis are coworkers. They're talking about the weekend. Did they like their weekend events? Listen."
- **Play 1 → Task A** (did they like it?). Pairs compare. Check together.
- **Read Tasks B and C together (1 min)** so learners know what to listen for.
- **Play 2 → Tasks B and C.** Pairs compare. Then show the answers (answer key).
- **"Say it" (2 min):** say: "*-ed* has 3 sounds." Read: relax**ed** (**t**), bor**ed** (**d**), interest**ed** (**id**, one more syllable). The class repeats each word twice. Then the lines in the box.
- **The audio plays the conversation once (about 1:15).** Press play again for play 2. A third play is fine if the class needs it.
- **If behind:** do Task C together on the board, reading the lines yourself.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:30–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 15 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.**
- **Show it (2 min):** draw on the board: a TV (the thing) → a face (the person). Under the TV write **boring**, under the face **bored**. Say: "**-ing** is the thing. **-ed** is the person's feeling."
- **Boxes 1 and 2 (4 min):** read a question from the left; the class answers from the right. Point out: ***It** was…* + *-ing*; ***I** was…* + *-ed*.
- **Box 3 (2 min):** an opinion + a reason: ***I thought it was** confusing, **so** I left early.*
- **Box 4 (1 min):** the -ing / -ed table; read across.
- **Check questions:** "The movie was ___ (bore)." (*boring*) • "I was very ___ (excite)." (*excited*) • "Were you ___ (interest)?" (*interested*) • "I thought it was ___ (confuse)." (*confusing*)
- **Practice A and B (3 min):** learners write, then pairs check. Go over the answers.
- **Pair drill (last 2 min):** A asks "How was your weekend?" B answers with one *-ing* and one *-ed* word (*It was relaxing. I was relaxed.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices it.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **How was it? It was… -ing / How did you feel? I was… -ed / I thought it was…, so…**
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · Talk Time: Star Ratings · 12 min
- **Grouping:** Round 1 pairs; Round 2 groups of 3–4. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 trio. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs, then 2 groups. 5 or fewer: one circle, then one group.
- **Round 1 (7 min):** each learner chooses an event card (1–6) or a real event (a TV show or a sports game is fine). A tells B about it: how it was, how they felt, and one reason. B asks **two follow-up questions** and gives it 1–5 stars in the table. Switch.
- **Model with one learner (1 min):** "Last Saturday, I went to the Fall Fair. It was exciting, but the lines were long, so I was tired." The learner asks: "Who did you go with? Was the food good?" → "4 stars!"
- **Round 2, groups (4 min):** each group chooses "the best weekend event" for the class bulletin board and agrees on two reasons (*It was exciting, and it was free.*). One person reports to the class.
- **If behind:** Round 1 only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:17 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner writes **two sentences** on a slip: one with *-ing* and one with *-ed* about the same event, and a reason with *because* or *so* (*The concert was exciting, so I was excited all night.*). Collect at the door.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (one sentence on the board). Say: "About 15 minutes. Bring it next week."
:::

:::step Read & Write · 7:17–7:25 · Three Short Reviews · 8 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Read (3 min):** learners read the three reviews and answer questions 1–5. Say: "Read fast first. Then find the answers."
- **Pairs check (1 min):** compare answers; check together.
- **Write (4 min):** learners write a 3–4 sentence review of an event (or a TV show, a movie, a game) with 1–5 stars and one reason.
- **If behind:** questions 1–5 only; the review becomes homework.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 4 sentences in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle **a** or **b**.
- **Parts B and C:** learners do them alone. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "With a little help." 😐 "Not yet."
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 13).
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 13 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Listening script: `L3_L11_listening.mp3` (about 1:15; the conversation plays once, so press play again for the second listen)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer, at a normal, friendly speed.

> **Narrator:** Lesson eleven. Listening. It's Monday morning. Elena and Luis are coworkers. They're talking about the weekend. Listen.
> **Luis:** Hi, Elena. How was your weekend? Did you do anything fun?
> **Elena:** It was great! I went to the food festival at Riverside Park with my sister.
> **Luis:** Really? How was it?
> **Elena:** It was so exciting. There was live music, and we tried food from ten countries.
> **Luis:** Was it crowded?
> **Elena:** Yes, very. The lines were long, so I was a little tired at the end. How about you?
> **Luis:** I went to a photo workshop at the city museum on Sunday. I was really interested in it.
> **Elena:** Oh, nice! Was it interesting?
> **Luis:** Not really. I thought it was confusing. The teacher talked very fast.
> **Elena:** Oh no. Were you bored?
> **Luis:** Yes, I was bored, so I left early. But the museum coffee shop was really relaxing.
> **Elena:** Next time, come to the festival with me. You'll love it!
> **Luis:** OK. That sounds fun. Maybe next month.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Listening Task A:* Elena (the food festival) **😀** • Luis (the photo workshop) **😐**.
- *Listening Task B:* 1 **c** (exciting) • 2 **a** (tired) • 3 **e** (confusing) • 4 **d** (bored) • 5 **b** (relaxing).
- *Listening Task C:* 1 exciting, 2 tired, 3 interesting, 4 confusing, 5 bored, 6 relaxing.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 boring, 2 bored, 3 interesting, 4 surprised, 5 tiring, 6 confused.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 exciting, 2 excited, 3 interested, 4 confusing, 5 relaxed.
- *Talk Time:* answers will vary.
- *Homework 1:* 1 The movie was boring. 2 I was tired after work. 3 The museum was interesting. 4 She was surprised. *Homework 2, 3, and 4:* answers will vary.
- *Read & Write B:* 1 the Fall Fair (5 stars) • 2 Music in the Park (2 stars) • 3 it was confusing; the teacher talked fast, but the library helper was patient • 4 the music started late, and it was too loud, so they were tired and left early • 5 the food (the apple pie) and the rides for kids • *Writing (C):* answers will vary.
- *Progress Check Part A (the teacher says, 2 times each):* 1 "The concert was boring." → **a** • 2 "I was surprised." → **b** • 3 "The museum was interesting." → **a** • 4 "We were tired, so we left." → **b**
- *Progress Check Part B:* 1 exciting, 2 bored, 3 confusing, 4 relaxed.
- *Progress Check Part C:* answers will vary; for example 1 *The festival was exciting. I was excited.* 2 *I thought it was boring, so I left early.*
- *Progress Check scoring:* A /4 + B /4 + C /2 = /10. This is not a test grade. It shows who needs more help hearing the endings (A) and choosing *-ing / -ed* (B, C).

### Teacher notes

- **General events only (important):** use community events, TV, movies, or sports. Learners never have to share where they spend their money or free time; the event cards are always an option.
- **The one rule:** *-ing* = the thing or the experience (*The movie was boring*). *-ed* = how the person feels (*I was bored*).
- **Careful:** *I'm boring* means people don't enjoy talking to me. Learners laugh when they hear it; use that.
- ***I thought it was…*** gives an opinion in the past: *I thought it was confusing, so I left early.* Accept *I think it was…* too.
- **-ed sounds:** /t/ after *x, k, s, sh, ch, p* (*relaxed*); /id/ after *t, d* (*interested*, one more syllable); /d/ for the rest (*bored, tired, surprised*). Don't teach the rule; just model and repeat.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *I'm boring* (for *bored*), *It was excited*, *I was interesting in the museum*, *The movie was bored*, *I thought was confusing*. Say the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the Spanish note and the tip on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain them.
- **Mixed levels:** stronger learners add a second reason and a recommendation (*You should go!*). Newer learners use Boxes 1 and 2 as a script.

### Grammar Box 1: *How was it?* (the thing: *-ing*)

<!-- subst -->

<!-- subst -->

|---|---|---|---|---|

### Grammar Box 4: *-ing* or *-ed*?

| THE THING: *It was…* | THE PERSON: *I was…* | THE THING: *It was…* | THE PERSON: *I was…* |
|---|---|---|---|
| exciting | excited | relaxing | relaxed |
| boring | bored | tiring | tired |
| interesting | interested | surprising | surprised |
| confusing | confused | | |

### Questions and answers

**Q: *I'm boring* or *I'm bored*?**

A: ***I'm bored*** = I feel bored. ***I'm boring*** = people don't have fun with me!

**Q: When do I use *-ing*?**

A: For the thing or the experience: ***The movie** was **boring**.*

**Q: When do I use *-ed*?**

A: For the person's feeling: ***I** was **bored**.*

**Q: How do I give an opinion?**

A: ***I thought it was*** + *-ing*, + a reason: *I thought it was exciting, **because** the music was great.*

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| I'm boring. (for a feeling) | I'm **bored**. |
| The movie was bored. | The movie was **boring**. |
| I was interesting in the museum. | I was **interested** in the museum. |
| I thought was confusing. | I thought **it** was confusing. |

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** en español es una palabra (*aburrido*); en inglés son dos: *I'm bored* (*estoy aburrido*), *It's boring* (*es aburrido*). Cuidado: *I'm boring* quiere decir *soy aburrido*.

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** people usually *feel* the *-ed* word. Things and experiences *are* the *-ing* word.
