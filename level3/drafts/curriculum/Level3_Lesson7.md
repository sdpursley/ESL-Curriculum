## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. Each Grammar page has one short Spanish note and one tip for speakers of other languages. Learners read them on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instructions:** use the same short task words every week: *Listen. Circle. Check. Write. Work with a partner. Work in a group. Ask a follow-up question.* Learners have the Task Words page from Lesson 1.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Do you write or speak? How many people do you talk to?"), **then go**. Say less, show more.

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Inside–outside circles:* the inner circle faces out, the outer circle faces in; the outer circle moves one person to the right each round. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

## Lesson 7 (Week 7): Can I Exchange This?

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- say *I'd like to return / exchange this* and give a reason (*because it's too small*)
- ask for permission with *Could I / Can I / May I…?* and answer
- ask for a refund or store credit, and ask for the manager politely
- change or cancel an appointment by phone
- tell the difference between *I'd like* + a thing and *I'd like to* + a verb
- read a store return policy and fill out a return form
:::

- Student Pages for Lesson 7 (print for the number expected + 3 spares)

- phone + small speaker, `L3_L7_listening.mp3`
- optional: a T-shirt or a bag to "return" when you model (no play money needed)
- **Prep time:** 10 min.
:::

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Opening Talk + Homework Check · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, the same partner for both parts. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **What will you do if…? If…, I'll…**
- **Opening Talk (6 min):** learners open the **Opening Talk** page. A asks a card question; B answers with 2–3 sentences; A asks one follow-up. Switch after each card.
- **Model with one learner:** "What will you do if it rains this weekend?" → (answer) → "**Really? With who?**"
- **Homework Check (4 min):** partners read their Lesson 6 Homework item 1 (*If it rains this weekend, I'll…*) to each other. Walk around. Write 2 common errors on the board (often *If it will rain* or *If rains*) and correct them together.
- **Late arrivals:** join any pair as a group of 3.
- **If behind:** one card only, then the homework check.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:18 · New Words · 8 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the New Words page.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat once. Hold up a T-shirt: "It's **too small**. I'd like to **return** it… or **exchange** it for a large."
- **Two pairs of words (1 min):** **refund** (money back) vs. **store credit** (money for this store only). **cancel** (no appointment) vs. **reschedule** (a new day or time).
- **Check meaning (1 min):** "The shirt has a brown spot. It has a…?" (*stain*) • "The phone doesn't work. It's…?" (*broken*) • "Who is the boss in the store?" (*the manager*) • "Thursday at 4:30 is free. It's…?" (*available*)
- **Pairs (2 min):** A says a word; B points to the picture and makes a sentence (*I'd like a refund.*). Switch.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:18–6:30 · Listening + "Say it" · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** point to the **customer** picture. Say: "This is Lina. She's at the service desk at Valley Mart, a big store. She has a phone case. What does she want to do? Listen."
- **Play 1 → Task A** (return or exchange? refund or store credit?). Pairs compare. Check together.
- **Read Tasks B and C together (1 min)** so learners know what to listen for.
- **Play 2 → Tasks B and C.** Pairs compare. Then show the answers (answer key).
- **"Say it" (2 min):** say: "Polite questions go **up** at the end: *Could I exchange it? ↗*" Then: "*I'd like to* is fast: **I'd-like-tuh**." Read the lines in the "Say it" box. The class repeats each line twice.
- **The audio plays the conversation once (about 1:15).** Press play again for play 2. A third play is fine if the class needs it.
- **If behind:** do Task C together on the board, reading the lines yourself.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:30–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 15 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Show the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.**
- **Write on the board:** **I'd like + a thing** (*I'd like **a bag***) and **I'd like to + a verb** (*I'd like **to return** it*).
- **Boxes 1 and 2 (4 min):** you are the clerk: "Can I help you?" The class answers from Box 1. Then "What's wrong with it?" The class answers from Box 2 (*It's too small. It has a stain.*).
- **Box 3 (3 min):** *Could I / Can I / May I…?* **May I** is the most polite. Read the answers: *Sure. / Of course. / Sorry, you need the receipt.*
- **Box 4 (2 min):** appointments. Read the phone lines across with one learner.
- **Check questions:** "I'd like ___ return this." (*to*) • "I'd like ___ refund." (*a*) • "It's ___ small. It doesn't fit." (*too*) • "___ I speak to the manager?" (*May / Could*)
- **Practice A and B (3 min):** learners write, then pairs check. Go over the answers.
- **Pair drill (last 2 min):** A is the clerk: "Can I help you?" B returns something from the New Words page with a reason. Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices it.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **I'd like to return / exchange this, because… / Could I…? / May I speak to the manager?**
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · Talk Time: Service Desk · 12 min
- **Grouping:** pairs: A = clerk, B = customer. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 trio (two customers wait in line). 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. 5 or fewer: one clerk; customers take turns.
- **Round 1 (6 min):** the clerk reads the **Store Policy Card** (30 days, receipt needed for a refund, store credit with no receipt, sale items store credit only). The customer chooses a **Customer Card** and returns or exchanges the item. The clerk follows the policy. After each card, switch roles.
- **Model with one learner (1 min):** you are the customer with card 1: "Hi. I'd like to return these shoes." → "What's wrong with them?" → "They're too big." → "Do you have the receipt?" → "Yes, here it is."
- **Round 2, phone call (5 min):** pairs sit **back to back** (no looking). A is the receptionist with the **available times**. B is the patient and wants to change or cancel an appointment. B uses the calendar slip. Then switch with the second slip.
- **If behind:** Round 1 only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:17 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner writes **two sentences** on a slip: one with *I'd like to…* and a reason with *because* (*I'd like to exchange this shirt, because it has a stain.*), and one polite question with *Could I* or *May I*. Collect at the door.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (one sentence on the board). Say: "About 15 minutes. Bring it next week. Next week is the midterm review."
:::

:::step Read & Write · 7:17–7:25 · A Return Policy and a Return Form · 8 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Read (3 min):** learners read the Valley Mart return policy and answer questions 1–5. Say: "Read fast first. Then find the answers."
- **Pairs check (1 min):** compare answers; check together.
- **Write (4 min):** learners read the situation and fill out the return form. They choose refund, exchange, or store credit (any choice that fits the policy is correct).
- **If behind:** questions 1–5 only; the form becomes homework.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 4 sentences in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle **a** or **b**.
- **Parts B and C:** learners do them alone. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "With a little help." 😐 "Not yet."
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 8).
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check at the start of the midterm review, while learners arrive.
:::

### Listening script: `L3_L7_listening.mp3` (about 1:17; the conversation plays once, so press play again for the second listen)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer, at a normal, friendly speed.

> **Narrator:** Lesson seven. Listening. Lina is at the service desk at Valley Mart. Listen.
> **Clerk:** Hi. Can I help you?
> **Lina:** Yes, I'd like to return this phone case.
> **Clerk:** OK. What's wrong with it?
> **Lina:** It's too small. It doesn't fit my phone.
> **Clerk:** I see. Do you have the receipt?
> **Lina:** No, sorry. I lost it. Could I get a refund?
> **Clerk:** Sorry, you need the receipt for a refund. But I can give you store credit.
> **Lina:** OK, that's fine. I'd like a bigger case.
> **Clerk:** Sure. The bigger cases are in aisle five.
> **Lina:** Thanks. Also, I have an appointment at your eye center on Monday. I'd like to change it.
> **Clerk:** No problem. I can do that here. What day is good for you?
> **Lina:** Is Thursday available?
> **Clerk:** Let me check. Yes, Thursday at four thirty is available.
> **Lina:** Perfect. I'd like to take that time.
> **Clerk:** OK. Your new appointment is Thursday at four thirty.
> **Lina:** Thank you so much.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Listening Task A:* 1 **a** (return it) • 2 **b** (store credit; she has no receipt).
- *Listening Task B:* 1 too small • 2 Monday • 3 Thursday • 4 4:30.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 like to, 2 Could, 3 like, 4 like to, 5 like to.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 like to, 2 like, 3 like to, 4 like, 5 like to.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 Could, 2 speak, 3 too small, 4 cancel, 5 available.
- *Talk Time Round 1 (following the Store Policy Card):* 1 shoes, too big, receipt, 10 days → refund or exchange • 2 lamp, broken, no receipt → store credit • 3 jacket on sale, stain → store credit only • 4 shirt, 45 days → sorry, more than 30 days (the customer can ask for the manager) • 5 coffee maker, wrong color, receipt, 5 days → refund or exchange • 6 pants, too long, no receipt → store credit or exchange. Wording will vary.
- *Talk Time Round 2:* Slip 1: Tuesday 10:00 → a new time on Wednesday or Friday (Wednesday 3:00, Friday 9:30, or Friday 4:30) • Slip 2: cancel the Thursday 2:00 appointment. Answers will vary.
- *Homework 1:* answers will vary; for example 1 I'd like to return these shoes, because they're too small. *Homework 2:* 1 like to, 2 like, 3 like to, 4 like, 5 like to, 6 like to. *Homework 3 and 4:* answers will vary.
- *Read & Write B:* 1 30 days • 2 store credit • 3 No (phones and electronics: 15 days only) • 4 No (no returns on food and medicine) • 5 store credit only
- *Read & Write C (sample form):* Item: a blue sweater, size M • Date bought: 10 days ago • Price: $24 • Reason: It has a stain. • Receipt: Yes • I'd like: a refund or an exchange (both fit the policy; store credit is also OK). Wording will vary.
- *Progress Check Part A (the teacher says, 2 times each):* 1 "I'd like to return these shoes. They're too big." → **a** • 2 "Could I exchange this shirt for a large?" → **b** • 3 "I'd like to cancel my appointment on Friday." → **a** • 4 "Is Tuesday at ten available? I'd like to change my appointment." → **b**
- *Progress Check Part B:* 1 to return, 2 exchange, 3 too small, 4 a refund.
- *Progress Check Part C:* answers will vary; for example 1 *I'd like to return this lamp, because it's broken.* 2 *May I speak to the manager, please?*
- *Progress Check scoring:* A /4 + B /4 + C /2 = /10. This is not a test grade. It shows who needs more help hearing requests (A) and with *I'd like / I'd like to* and polite questions (B, C).

### Teacher notes

- **Invented stores only (important):** Valley Mart, its policy, and the eye center are invented. No play money is needed. Learners never talk about their own purchases, debts, or money problems.
- ***I'd like* vs. *I'd like to*:** after *I'd like*, use a thing (*I'd like a bag*). After *I'd like to*, use a verb (*I'd like to return it*). *I'd like* = *I would like*: more polite than *I want*.
- ***too* + adjective:** *It's **too** small* means it's a problem (it doesn't fit). *very small* is not a problem. Teach *too small / too big / too long* as fixed phrases.
- ***Could I / Can I / May I***: all are OK. *May I* is the most polite and formal. The answer is usually *Sure. / Of course. / Sorry, …*.
- **Midterm next week:** remind learners. The midterm reviews Lessons 1–7.
- **Mixed levels:** stronger learners argue politely in Round 1 (*Could I speak to the manager, please?*). Newer learners use Boxes 1–3 as a script.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *I'd like return*, *I like to return this* (for now), *Could I to exchange*, *It's very small* (for a problem), *I want change my appointment*. Say the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the Spanish note and the tip on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain them.

<!-- subst -->

<!-- subst -->
| *I'd like* + A THING | ‖ | *I'd like to* + A VERB |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like **a refund**. | ‖ | I'd like **to return** this. |
| I'd like **a bigger size**. | ‖ | I'd like **to exchange** it. |
| I'd like **an appointment** on Friday. | ‖ | I'd like **to cancel** my appointment. |

### Questions and answers

**Q: *I'd like* or *I want*?**

A: *I'd like* (= *I would like*) is **more polite**. Use it in stores, at the clinic, and on the phone.

**Q: *I'd like* or *I'd like to*?**

A: A **thing** after *I'd like* (*I'd like a bag*). A **verb** after *I'd like to* (*I'd like to return it*).

**Q: *too small* or *very small*?**

A: ***too small*** = a problem: it doesn't fit. *very small* = not a problem. *It's **too** small, so I'd like to exchange it.*

**Q: *Could I*, *Can I*, or *May I*?**

A: All three are OK. *May I* is the most polite. After them, use the base verb: *Could I **exchange** it?* (not *to exchange*)

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| I'd like return this. | I'd like **to** return this. |
| I want change my appointment. | I'd like **to** change my appointment. |
| Could I to exchange it? | Could I **exchange** it? |
| It's very small. It doesn't fit. | It's **too** small. It doesn't fit. |
| I'd like to a refund. | I'd like **a** refund. |

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** *I'd like to return this* = *Quisiera devolver esto.* *return* = *devolver*; *exchange* = *cambiar*. *May I…?* es muy cortés, como *¿Me permite…?*

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** after *I'd like* use a thing (*I'd like a bag*). After *I'd like to* use a verb (*I'd like to return it*).

:::box Store Policy Card (for the clerk)
