# Image Credits and Licenses: Level 3, Lesson 13

**13 picture files** for Lesson 13: 12 vocabulary cards for the New Words page and 1 activity picture(s) for the student pages. Every file is a PNG made by `tools/make_images_L3_L13.py` with the card helpers in `tools/l3img.py` (the same helpers and card style as the Level 1 and Level 2 `make_images.py` and Level 3 Lesson 1). Vocabulary cards are 600×420.
