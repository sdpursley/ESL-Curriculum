## How to Use This Packet

- **The test for learners is a separate file:** `Level3_Midterm_Student_Test.pdf` (no answers, no cover page). Print 1 per learner + 3 extra.
- **This packet** is for you: directions, the listening script, the answer key, the scoring guide, and the speaking papers.
- **Audio:** the recording `L3_Midterm_listening.mp3` is not ready yet (the script is about 1:39 long). Until it is ready, read the listening script aloud two times, at a natural speed. Two readers are better: one reads the women's lines and one reads the men's lines.
- **All instructions are in English.** Read each instruction aloud once and show an example on the board if learners look unsure.

## Week 8: Midterm: Step by Step

### What the test has

| Part | What learners do | Points | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Listening | Listen to two short conversations (a small car accident; a return at a store). Circle an answer, mark true or false, or write one word (10 items). | 10 | 15 min (6:10–6:25) |
| B. Words | Complete sentences with words from a box (8 items). | 8 | 20 min (6:25–6:45) for B + C |
| C. Grammar | Part 1: circle the correct words (15 items). Part 2: find and fix one mistake in each sentence (5 items). | 20 | (with B) |
| D. Reading | Read a text of about 100 words. Answer 6 questions. | 6 | 10 min (7:00–7:10) |
| E. Writing | Choose one of two tasks. Write 4–5 sentences. | 6 | 15 min (7:10–7:25) |
| **Written total** | | **50** | **60 min** |
| Speaking interview | Pairs with the teacher, during the written parts. Scored with the rubric, not added to the written total. | 3–9 | 4 min per pair |

*Covers Lessons 1–7. Listening: a small car accident (Lesson 4), an offer of help (Lesson 5) and a return at a service desk (Lesson 7). Grammar: because/so, look like/be like, object pronouns, used to, past continuous with when/while, I'll and going to, If + present, I'd like to. Reading: an incident report at work. Writing: an accident report or a text that offers help.*

### Before class (20 minutes)

1. Print the student test (1 per learner + 3). From this packet, print the Speaking Task Cards (1 set per pair), the Score Sheet, and the Self-Check (1 per learner).
2. The recording is not ready yet: ask a second reader to help, and read the listening script through once before class.
3. Set 2 chairs and 1 chair for you in a quiet corner (for the speaking interviews).
4. Have pencils, a clock or timer, and the class list.

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Welcome and warm-up · 10 min
- **Say:** "Tonight is the test. First listening, then words and grammar, then a break, then reading and writing. I will call you in pairs for a short speaking interview."
- Warm-up: pairs ask each other 2 questions from the board (What did you do last weekend? What are you going to do next month?). Call the first pair for the interview.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:25 · A. Listening · 15 min
- Hand out the test. Learners write their name. Read the Part A instruction and the scene line aloud.
- Give 1 minute to read the questions. Play the audio (or read the script) **once**. Wait 1 minute. Play it **again**. Give 2 more minutes.
- No interviews during the listening.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:25–6:45 · B. Words and C. Grammar · 20 min
- Read the Part B and Part C instructions aloud. Show Part C2 on the board: *She are my neighbor.* → *She is my neighbor.*
- Learners work alone and quietly. Call pairs for the speaking interview (4 minutes per pair).
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:45–7:00 · Break · 15 min
- Collect nothing yet: learners turn the test over. Continue interviews if needed.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · D. Reading · 10 min
- Read the instruction. Learners read the text and answer. Continue interviews.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:25 · E. Writing · 15 min
- Read both tasks aloud. Learners check the box for the task they chose and write 4–5 sentences.
- Say: "Give a reason. Use because or so." At 7:23, say: "Two more minutes. Read your writing one time."
:::

:::step Step 7 · 7:25–7:30 · Self-check and goodbye · 5 min
- Collect the tests. Hand out the Can-Do Self-Check. Learners check the boxes and write one goal. Collect it.
- Finish any last interviews after class or at the start of the next class.
:::

### Clock-time plan

| Time | Whole class | Teacher |
|---|---|---|
| 6:00–6:10 | Warm-up questions in pairs | Interview pair 1 |
| 6:10–6:25 | A. Listening | Play the audio two times |
| 6:25–6:45 | B. Words, C. Grammar | Interviews |
| 6:45–7:00 | Break | Interviews if needed |
| 7:00–7:10 | D. Reading | Interviews |
| 7:10–7:25 | E. Writing | Interviews |
| 7:25–7:30 | Self-check | Collect everything |

:::box Speaking interview (4 minutes per pair)
- **Part 1 (about 2 minutes).** Ask each learner 2 of the questions on the Speaking Interview Card. After each answer, ask one follow-up question (Why? When? Who with? What happened next?).
- **Part 2 (about 2 minutes).** Give the pair one Task Card. They read it for 20 seconds and then talk to each other. You listen and only help if they stop.
- **Score** each learner on the 3 rubric areas (1–3 each, 3–9 in total). Write the score at once, before the next pair.
:::

### Speaking rubric (1–3 in each area)

| Area | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Communication | Short answers only; needs a lot of help. | Answers, but gives no reason or asks no question. | Gives reasons **and** asks or answers a follow-up question. |
| Target grammar | Few lesson forms; many mistakes stop the meaning. | Uses some lesson forms (used to, was …-ing, I'll, If …); mistakes do not stop the meaning. | Uses lesson forms correctly most of the time. |
| Pronunciation and fluency | Hard to understand; long stops. | Understandable with some effort; some stops. | Easy to understand; few stops. |

*From Level 3 Scope §7. Score 3–9 in total. A learner who gives a reason and then asks the partner "What about you?" or "Why?" shows Communication 3.*

## Listening Script

### Listening script: `L3_Midterm_listening.mp3` (about 1:39, estimated from the script; not recorded yet; play it two times)

Speakers: Clerk, David, Elena, and a narrator.

> **Narrator:** Midterm listening. Part one. Elena and David are neighbors. Elena is telling David about her morning. Listen.
> **David:** Hi, Elena. Are you OK? You look tired.
> **Elena:** I had a small accident this morning. I'm OK, but my car isn't.
> **David:** Oh no! What happened?
> **Elena:** I was waiting at the traffic light on Pine Street when a truck hit my car.
> **David:** Was the driver looking at his phone?
> **Elena:** Yes, he was. No one was hurt, but there's a big dent in the back of my car.
> **David:** Did you call the police?
> **Elena:** Yes. A police officer came, and she wrote a report.
> **David:** You should call the insurance company today.
> **Elena:** I know. I'm going to call them after lunch.
> **David:** I'll drive you to work tomorrow.
> **Elena:** Thanks, David! That's very kind.
> **Narrator:** Part two. The next day, Elena is at the service desk at Valley Mart.
> **Clerk:** Hi. Can I help you?
> **Elena:** Yes. I'd like to return this jacket. It's too small, and it has a stain.
> **Clerk:** Do you have the receipt?
> **Elena:** Yes, here it is. Could I exchange it for a large?
> **Clerk:** Sorry, we don't have a large in blue.
> **Elena:** Then I'd like a refund, please.
> **Clerk:** Sure. Here's your money. Have a nice day!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

## Scoring Guide

### Written test: score bands (proposed)

| Written score | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 39–50 | **Meets** | The learner can use most of the Lesson 1–7 language on their own. |
| 26–38 | **Almost** | The learner understands a lot but still needs practice. Look at which part was low. |
| 0–25 | **Not yet** | Talk with the learner. Plan extra practice before the final. |

*These bands are **proposed** (about 78% and 52%, the same as the Level 1 and Level 2 exams). The program confirms them after the first classes use the test.*

### Writing rubric (Part E, 6 points)

| Area | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task | Off topic or fewer than 3 sentences. | On topic; 3 sentences, or a part of the task is missing. | Does the task in 4–5 sentences, with a reason. |
| Grammar | Many mistakes; the meaning is hard to follow. | Some mistakes in the lesson forms. | Uses the lesson forms for the task with few mistakes. |
| Clear for a reader | A reader cannot follow it. | A reader understands most of it after reading it two times. | A reader understands it on the first reading. |

*Spelling mistakes do not lose points if the word is clear. Score what the learner wrote, not what is missing from the model answer.*

### Speaking (3–9, separate from the written score)

- 7–9: strong for this level. 5–6: on track. 3–4: needs more speaking practice. *(Proposed.)*

### After the midterm

- Give back the tests in the next class. Go over Part C together: learners correct their own mistakes.
- For learners in the **Not yet** band, choose 2 lessons to review with them (the lesson numbers are in the table above).
- The final exam is in Week 15 and covers all 13 lessons, with more items from Lessons 8–13.

## Teacher Notes

- Midterm: the listening has two parts (Elena and her neighbor; Elena at a store). Read the scene line aloud before you play it.
- Part C2: accept any correct sentence that fixes the one mistake (for example, "I talk to her every day").
- All test items use only words and grammar taught in the Level 3 lessons. If a learner missed a lesson, look at the lesson number in the answer key before you decide what to review.
- Learners may ask what a word in an instruction means. You may explain the instruction, but not the words that are tested.
- Learners who need more time can finish Part E at the start of the next class.

## Papers to Copy

### Speaking Interview Card (Teacher)

**Part 1. Ask each learner 2 questions. Then ask one follow-up question.**

1. Tell me about your week.
2. What did you do last weekend?
3. What are you going to do next month?
4. Describe a friend. What does he or she look like? What is he or she like?
5. What was your life like as a child? What did you use to do?
6. What will you do if it rains on Saturday?

**Follow-up questions:** Why? When was that? Who were you with? What happened next? How did you feel?

**Part 2. Give the pair one Task Card.** Listen for:

- **Card A (Return an item):** I'd like to…, Could I…?, a clear reason (it's too big / it has a stain), polite answers.
- **Card B (A storm plan):** If + present, will / 'll, probably, should, because; each learner gives at least one idea and answers the other.
