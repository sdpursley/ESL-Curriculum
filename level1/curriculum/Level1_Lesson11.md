## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it**, **check it**, **then go**. Same short words: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* neighbor (odd → one group of 3). *Groups:* 3–4. *Mingle:* stand and talk to many classmates.

> **When a learner is stuck:** show the picture or board → one short English example → point to the grammar box → classmate helps briefly, then English again.

> **Invented streets only.** Use the map on the page. Never send learners into a real street alone for homework.

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **Is there a kitchen? Where's the bank? Go straight.**
- **Model with one learner:** "Is there a kitchen?" → "Yes, there is." Then mime: "Turn left."
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show last week's homework. Walk around and look.
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** point to map places: "**Bank**. **Pharmacy**. **Park**. **School**." Mime: "**Go straight**. **Turn left**. **Turn right**."
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. ★ words twice more.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then A asks *Where's the ___?* B answers with *next to / across from*.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Ana is on the street. She asks Marco for directions to the **pharmacy**."
- **Listen 1 → Task A.** **Listen 2 → Task B.** **Listen 3 → Task C.**
- **Pairs compare** after each task. Write answers on the board.
- **Say it:** class repeats: *Go straight two blocks.* and *It's next to the bank.*
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play 2 times; do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** Grammar page. **Left = question. Right = answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Box 1:** *Where's the bank? → It's on Main Street.*
- **Box 2:** *It's next to / across from / between…*
- **Box 3:** *Go straight. Turn left. Turn right.* Mime each one.
- **Check questions:** "The pharmacy is ___ the bank." (*next to*) • "Turn ___ at the corner." (*left*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A asks *Where's the…?* B answers from the map; then A gives one direction.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same language.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **Where's the…? It's next to… Go straight. Turn left. Turn right.** Put map pages ready.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time · 10 min
- **Grouping:** A–B pairs. **Student A has the map** (does not show it at first if you want an information gap).
- **Part 1 (5 min):** **B** asks *Where's the ___?* **A** answers from the map (*next to / across from / between*). Three places.
- **Part 2 (4 min):** **B** asks for directions to one place; **A** says *Go straight… Turn…* Then **switch**.
- **If behind:** one round / half of Talk Time only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner says *Where's the ___?* or *Go straight / Turn left.*
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 line 1 together. Point to each instruction.
:::

:::step Real-World Reading · 7:15–7:25 · Street Map + Signs · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Real-World Reading page.
- **See it (2 min):** Task A — find places on the map.
- **Hear it (2 min):** say *Turn left*; learners point to LEFT sign / picture.
- **Read it (4 min):** Tasks B–C — signs (EXIT, ONE WAY, PUSH); follow one written direction.
- **Partners:** A gives a direction; B points on the map.
- **Learners who can't read yet:** they point to pictures and say key words with everyone.
- **If behind:** Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the items in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** match and write on their own. Help with instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line; learners check one face.
- **Collect if you want.** These checks are light practice, not a test grade.
- **If behind:** do it next week at 6:00.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L11_listening.mp3` (about 1:10; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Invented streets (Main Street). No real city names required.

> **Narrator:** Lesson eleven. Listening. Ana is on the street. She asks Marco for directions to the pharmacy. Listen.
> **Ana:** Excuse me. Where's the pharmacy?
> **Marco:** It's on Main Street.
> **Ana:** How do I get there?
> **Marco:** Go straight two blocks.
> **Ana:** Go straight two blocks. OK.
> **Marco:** Then turn left at the corner. It's next to the bank.
> **Ana:** Turn left at the corner. Next to the bank.
> **Marco:** Yes. It's across from the park.
> **Ana:** Across from the park. Is it near the school?
> **Marco:** Yes, it is. The school is on the same street, between the park and the store.
> **Ana:** Between the park and the store. Thank you so much!
> **Marco:** You're welcome. Have a nice day!
> **Ana:** You too. Bye!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

**Listening A:** 1 left  2 bank  3 park. **B:** Main Street. **C:** Go straight; Turn left (not Turn right).
**Grammar practice:** 1 across from (or next to)  2 across from  3 between  4 left  5 left (or right)  6 answers will vary
**Real-World Reading A:** 1 pharmacy  2 pharmacy. **B:** EXIT–b  ONE WAY–c  PUSH–a. **C:** 1 Go straight  2 Turn left. **D:** partner practice (no written key).
**Progress Check A** (teacher reads twice): 1 bank  2 left  3 park  4 pharmacy  5 school
**B:** 1–c  2–d  3–b  4–a
**C:** 1 across from  2 between  3 next to
**Scoring:** A /5 + B /4 + C /3 = /12. Not a test grade.

### Teacher notes

- Invented streets. Homework: look at a map on paper or phone — do **not** require walking alone.
- **Big mistake this week:** *in the left* / mixing *next to* and *across from*. Mime and point.
- Left/right: stand with the class facing the same way before you mime.
- Learners who can't read yet: they point on the map and say place names.

### Grammar Box 1: *Where's the…?*

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Excuse me, where's the | bank? / pharmacy? | ‖ | It's **on** | Main Street. |
| ^ | ^ | ‖ | It's **next to** | the pharmacy. |

### Grammar Box 2: *across from / between*

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where's the | park? | ‖ | It's **across from** | the bank. |
| Where's the | store? | ‖ | It's **between** | the bank and the school. |

### Grammar Box 3: Directions

<!-- subst -->
| SAY | < | ‖ | SAY | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Go | straight. | ‖ | Turn | **left**. / **right**. |
| It's on your | left. / right. | ‖ | | |

---
### Grammar Box 4: *Excuse me* + directions

<!-- subst -->
| YOU | < | ‖ | HELPER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Excuse me. | Where's the pharmacy? | ‖ | It's on | Main Street. |
| How do I | get there? | ‖ | Go straight | two blocks. Turn left. |

### Grammar Box 5: *near* / *on the same street*

<!-- subst -->
| SAY | < | ‖ | DON'T SAY | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| It's **near** the school. |  | ‖ | It's | nearly the school. |
| It's **on** Main Street. |  | ‖ | It's | in Main Street. |

### Questions and answers

**Q: *on* or *in* the street?**
A: *on Main Street* (not *in Main Street*).

**Q: *Turn left* — do I say *to the*?**
A: No. *Turn left.* (not *Turn to the left* in this class).


**Q: *Where is* or *Where's*?**
A: Both OK. *Where's* = *Where is* (fast speech).

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| in Main Street | **on** Main Street |
| Turn to the left. | Turn **left**. |
| It's near of the bank. | It's **next to** the bank. |

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** Read the grammar boxes left to right. Copy one question and one answer.

*Nota para hispanohablantes: *Turn left* (sin *to the*). *on Main Street* (no *in*). *next to* = *al lado de*.*
