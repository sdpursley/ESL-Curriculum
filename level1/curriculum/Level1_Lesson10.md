## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it**, **check it**, **then go**. Same short words: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* neighbor (odd → one group of 3). *Groups:* 3–4. *Mingle:* stand and talk to many classmates.

> **When a learner is stuck:** show the picture or board → one short English example → point to the grammar box → classmate helps briefly, then English again.

> **Never ask real rent or real addresses.** Learners describe a dream home or an invented apartment.

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **When is your birthday? How's the weather today? Is there a kitchen?**
- **Model with one learner:** "When is your birthday?" → "It's in May." Then "Is there a kitchen in your home?"
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show last week's homework. Walk around and look.
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** point to the floor plan: "**Kitchen**. **Bedroom**. **Bathroom**. **Living room**." Hold up furniture cards: "**Bed**. **Table**. **Chair**. **Sofa**."
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. ★ words twice more.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then A asks *Is there a ___?* B answers *Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.*
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Rosa calls about an **apartment**. She asks: **Is there…? Are there…?**"
- **Listen 1 → Task A.** **Listen 2 → Task B.** **Listen 3 → Task C.**
- **Pairs compare** after each task. Write answers on the board.
- **Say it:** class repeats: *There are two bedrooms.* and *Is there a washing machine?*
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play 2 times; do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** Grammar page. **Left = question. Right = answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Box 1:** *There is a kitchen. There are two bedrooms.* Board: **one → is; two or more → are.**
- **Box 2:** *Is there a sofa? → Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.*
- **Box 3:** *Are there windows? How many bedrooms are there? → There are two.*
- **Check questions:** "There ___ a table." (*is*) • "There ___ two chairs." (*are*) • "___ there a sofa?" (*Is*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A looks at the floor plan; B asks *Is there…? Are there…? How many…?* Switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same language.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **Is there a…? Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. Are there…? How many… are there?** Put floor-plan pages ready.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time · 10 min
- **Grouping:** A–B pairs. Partners don't show the floor plan if A keeps it.
- **Part 1 — Floor plan (6 min):** **A** looks at the plan. **B** asks and draws or writes. Then compare.
- **Part 2 — Dream home (3 min):** each learner says 3 sentences: *There is… There are…*
- **If behind:** one round / half of Talk Time only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner says *There is a ___.* or *There are ___.* Copying is fine.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 line 1 together. Point to each instruction.
:::

:::step Real-World Reading · 7:15–7:25 · Apartment Ad · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Real-World Reading page.
- **See it (2 min):** Task A — point to BR, BA, price on the ad.
- **Hear it (2 min):** say 2 BR, 1 BA; learners point.
- **Read it (4 min):** Tasks B–C — abbreviations; yes/no about the ad.
- **Partners:** A asks *How many bedrooms?* B answers from the ad.
- **Learners who can't read yet:** they point to pictures and say key words with everyone.
- **If behind:** Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the items in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** match and write on their own. Help with instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line; learners check one face.
- **Collect if you want.** These checks are light practice, not a test grade.
- **If behind:** do it next week at 6:00.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L10_listening.mp3` (about 1:10; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Invented apartment. Never ask learners for real rent.

> **Narrator:** Lesson ten. Listening. Rosa calls about an apartment. She talks to the rental agent. Listen.
> **Agent:** Hello. Green Street Apartments.
> **Rosa:** Hi. Is there an apartment for rent?
> **Agent:** Yes, there is. There are two bedrooms and one bathroom.
> **Rosa:** Is there a washing machine?
> **Agent:** No, there isn't. But there is a refrigerator in the kitchen.
> **Rosa:** OK. Are there windows in the kitchen?
> **Agent:** Yes, there are. There are two windows.
> **Rosa:** How many bedrooms are there?
> **Agent:** There are two bedrooms. The living room is big.
> **Rosa:** Is the living room big?
> **Agent:** Yes, it is. The living room is big.
> **Rosa:** When is the rent due?
> **Agent:** The rent is due on the first of the month.
> **Rosa:** OK. Thank you. Goodbye.
> **Agent:** Goodbye. Have a nice day!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

**Listening A:** two bedrooms ✓; washing machine ✗; refrigerator ✓; windows in the kitchen ✓; big living room ✓. **B:** bedrooms 2; bathrooms 1. **C:** on the first.
**Grammar practice:** 1 is  2 are  3 Is  4 Are  5 are  6–7 answers will vary
**Homework 2:** 1 is  2 are  3 is  4 are
**Real-World Reading A:** 2; 1; $900. **B:** 2 BR → b bedrooms; 1 BA → a bathroom; /mo → c per month
**C:** 1 Yes  2 Yes. **D:** partner practice (no written key).
**Progress Check A** (teacher reads twice): 1 apartment  2 kitchen  3 sofa  4 bedroom  5 window
**B:** 1–b  2–c  3–a  4–d
**C:** 1 is  2 are  3 Is  4 Are
**Scoring:** A /5 + B /4 + C /4 = /13. Not a test grade.

### Teacher notes

- Invented apartment and ad. **Never ask real rent or real addresses.**
- Dream home is fine for Talk Time and homework.
- **Big mistake this week:** *Have two bedrooms* / *There is two beds*. Point to Grammar Box 1.
- Spanish *hay* = *there is / there are* (two English forms).
- Learners who can't write yet: they point on the floor plan and say *Yes, there is.*

### Grammar Box 1: *There is / There are*

<!-- subst -->
| ONE | < | ‖ | TWO OR MORE | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| There **is** | a kitchen. / a bed. | ‖ | There **are** | two bedrooms. / chairs. |

### Grammar Box 2: *Is there…?*

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Is** there | a sofa? / a washing machine? | ‖ | Yes, | there **is**. |
| ^ | ^ | ‖ | No, | there **isn't**. |

### Grammar Box 3: *Are there…? How many…?*

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Are** there | windows? | ‖ | Yes, there **are**. / No, there **aren't**. |
| How many bedrooms | are there? | ‖ | There are | two. |

### Grammar Box 4: short answers

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | ‖ | SHORT ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Is there a bed? |  | ‖ | Yes, | there is. / No, there isn't. |
| Are there chairs? |  | ‖ | Yes, | there are. / No, there aren't. |

### Grammar Box 5: rooms + *in the*

<!-- subst -->
| SAY | < | ‖ | EXAMPLE | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| There is a table | **in the** kitchen. | ‖ | There are two windows | **in the** bedroom. |

### Questions and answers

**Q: *is* or *are*?**
A: One thing → *There is… / Is there…?* Two or more → *There are… / Are there…?*

**Q: How is this different from Spanish?**
A: Spanish *hay* is one word. English has two forms: *there is* and *there are*.


**Q: Do I say *a* with *there is*?**
A: Yes for one countable thing: *There is a bed.*

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| Have two bedrooms. | **There are** two bedrooms. |
| There is two beds. | There **are** two beds. |
| Is there windows? | **Are** there windows? |

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** Read the grammar boxes left to right. Copy one question and one answer.

*Nota para hispanohablantes: *There is / There are* = *hay* (una sola palabra en español). Di **There are two bedrooms** (no «Have two bedrooms»).*
