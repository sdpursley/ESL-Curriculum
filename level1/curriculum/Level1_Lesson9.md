## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it**, **check it**, **then go**. Same short words: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* neighbor (odd → one group of 3). *Groups:* 3–4. *Mingle:* stand and talk to many classmates.

> **When a learner is stuck:** show the picture or board → one short English example → point to the grammar box → classmate helps briefly, then English again.

> **Birthdays only — no holidays.** Learners may use an invented birthday. Never ask about religious or cultural holidays.

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **Do you like fish? What do you want for lunch? What day is today?**
- **Model with one learner:** "Do you like fish?" → "Yes, I do." Then "What day is today?"
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show last week's homework. Walk around and look.
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** point to a calendar: "**Month**. **Date**. **Today**. **Tomorrow**." Mime weather: "**Sunny**. **Rainy**. **Cold**. **Hot**." Show ordinals: "**first**, **second**, **third**…"
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. ★ words twice more.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then A asks *When is your birthday?* (invented OK).
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Kim calls the **clinic**. She makes an **appointment**. Then Kim and Marco talk about the **weather** and **birthdays**."
- **Listen 1 → Task A.** **Listen 2 → Task B.** **Listen 3 → Task C.**
- **Pairs compare** after each task. Write answers on the board.
- **Say it:** class repeats: *Thursday, May tenth, at ten thirty.* and *How's the weather?*
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play 2 times; do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** Grammar page. **Left = question. Right = answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Box 1:** *What's the date today? → It's October 20th.*
- **Box 2:** *When is your birthday? → It's on May 5th. / It's in May.* Board: **on** + date/day; **in** + month.
- **Box 3:** *When is your appointment? → On Tuesday at 3:00.* (**at** + time)
- **Box 4:** *How's the weather? → It's sunny / rainy / cold / hot.* Cross out *It makes cold.*
- **Check questions:** "My birthday is ___ May." (*in*) • "My birthday is ___ May 5th." (*on*) • "Class is ___ 6:00." (*at*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A asks birthday / appointment / weather; B answers; switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same language.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **When is your birthday? It's on… / in… How's the weather? Your appointment is on… at…** Prepare A/B Talk Time pages.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class for the birthday line; then A–B pairs for the appointment.
- **Part 1 — Birthday line (5 min):** learners line up by birthday month/day by **asking only** (*When is your birthday?*). Invented birthdays OK. Model with 2 learners first.
- **Part 2 — Appointment (5 min):** **A = receptionist**, **B = patient** with the appointment card; then **switch**.
- **If behind:** one round / half of Talk Time only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner says *My birthday is in ___.* or *It's on ___.* Copying from the box is fine.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 line 1 together. Point to each instruction.
:::

:::step Real-World Reading · 7:15–7:25 · Calendar + Appointment Card · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Real-World Reading page.
- **See it (2 min):** Task A — point to day, date, time on the appointment card.
- **Hear it (2 min):** say 3 dates; learners point or write.
- **Read it (4 min):** Tasks B–C — U.S. date order (5/10 = May 10); write today's date two ways.
- **Partners:** A is receptionist, B repeats the appointment.
- **Learners who can't read yet:** they point to pictures and say key words with everyone.
- **If behind:** Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the items in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** match and write on their own. Help with instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line; learners check one face.
- **Collect if you want.** These checks are light practice, not a test grade.
- **If behind:** do it next week at 6:00.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L9_listening.mp3` (about 1:10; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Say dates as words: *May tenth, May fifth, ten thirty.*

> **Narrator:** Lesson nine. Listening. Part one. Kim calls the clinic. She makes an appointment. Listen.
> **Receptionist:** Good morning. Riverside Clinic.
> **Kim:** Hi. I need an appointment, please.
> **Receptionist:** OK. How about Thursday, May tenth, at ten thirty?
> **Kim:** Thursday, May tenth, at ten thirty. Is that in the morning?
> **Receptionist:** Yes, it is. Ten thirty in the morning.
> **Kim:** OK. Thank you!
> **Receptionist:** You're welcome. See you Thursday.
> **Narrator:** Part two. Kim and Marco talk about the weather and birthdays. Listen.
> **Kim:** Hi, Marco. How's the weather today?
> **Marco:** It's sunny and hot. I like sunny days.
> **Kim:** Me too. When is your birthday?
> **Marco:** It's on May fifth. Next week!
> **Kim:** Oh, soon! My birthday is in October.
> **Marco:** Nice. See you later, Kim!
> **Kim:** Bye, Marco!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

**Listening A:** Thursday; May 10; 10:30 (morning). **B:** sunny, hot. **C:** Marco — May 5th; Kim — October.
**Grammar practice:** 1 on  2 in  3 at  4 sunny (or rainy / cold / hot)  5 on  6 answers will vary (today's date)
**Homework 2:** 1 in  2 on  3 at  4 on
**Real-World Reading A:** Thursday; May 10; 10:30. **B:** 5/10 → b May 10; 10/5 → a October 5; 12/25 → c December 25
**C:** answers will vary (today's date). **D:** partner practice (no written key).
**Progress Check A** (teacher reads twice each): 1 May  2 sunny  3 10:30  4 cold  5 today
**B:** 1–b  2–a  3–c  4–d
**C:** 1 in  2 on  3 at  4 on
**Scoring:** A /5 + B /4 + C /4 = /13. Not a test grade.

### Teacher notes

- Invented clinic (Riverside Clinic) and dates. U.S. date order: month/day/year.
- **No holidays.** Never ask about religious or cultural holidays. Invented birthdays are fine.
- Ordinals 1st–31st: teach with the card; Progress Check uses common ones.
- Weather: *It's cold* (not *It makes cold*); *I'm cold* for people.
- **Big mistake this week:** *in May 5th* / *at May*. Point to Grammar Box 2.
- Learners who can't write yet: they say the month and point to the calendar.

### Questions and answers

**Q: *in*, *on*, or *at*?**
A: **in** + month (*in May*). **on** + date or day (*on May 5th*, *on Tuesday*). **at** + time (*at 3:00*).

**Q: How do I write a date in the U.S.?**
A: Month / day / year. *5/10* = May 10 (not October 5).


**Q: Do months start with a capital letter?**
A: Yes. *May*, *October* (always).

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| It makes cold. | It's **cold**. |
| I have cold. | I'm cold. / It's cold. |
| in May 5th | **on** May 5th |
| at May | **in** May |

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** Read the grammar boxes left to right. Copy one question and one answer.

*Nota para hispanohablantes: En EE. UU. la fecha es mes/día/año (5/10 = 10 de mayo). Los meses llevan mayúscula. *It's cold* (no «It makes cold»).*
