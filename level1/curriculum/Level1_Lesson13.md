## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it**, **check it**, **then go**. Same short words: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* neighbor (odd → one group of 3). *Groups:* 3–4. *Mingle:* stand and talk to many classmates.

> **When a learner is stuck:** show the picture or board → one short English example → point to the grammar box → classmate helps briefly, then English again.

> **Invented interview.** Learners may invent skills. Graduation talk: 3 sentences is fine; reading from paper is fine.

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **How do you feel? Can you cook? Can you drive?**
- **Model with one learner:** "How do you feel?" → "I feel fine." Then "Can you cook?" → "Yes, I can."
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show last week's homework. Walk around and look.
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** mime skills: "**Cook**. **Drive**. **Clean**. **Lift**." Hold up: "**Computer**. **Speak English**."
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. ★ words twice more.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then A asks *Can you ___?* B answers *Yes, I can. / No, I can't.*
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Ana has a **job interview** at a restaurant. The manager asks: **Can you…?**"
- **Listen 1 → Task A.** **Listen 2 → Task B.** **Listen 3 → Task C.**
- **Pairs compare** after each task. Write answers on the board.
- **Say it:** class repeats: *Can you work on weekends?* and *Yes, I can.*
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play 2 times; do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** Grammar page. **Left = question. Right = answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Box 1:** *Can you cook? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.*
- **Box 2:** *I can drive. I can't swim.*
- **Box 3:** interview questions: *Can you work on weekends? Can you start on Monday?*
- Cross out *I can to cook* and *I can cooking.*
- **Check:** "___ you drive?" (*Can*) • "No, I ___." (*can't*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A asks 4 *Can you…?* questions from the skill cards; B answers; switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same language.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **Can you…? Yes, I can. / No, I can't. I can… I can't…** Put interview A/B pages ready.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time · 10 min
- **Grouping:** A–B pairs.
- **Part 1 — Skills survey (4 min):** ask 3 classmates *Can you…?* Write ✓ or ✗.
- **Part 2 — Interview (5 min):** **A = manager**, **B = applicant**; then **switch**. Use the job-interview page.
- Remind: invent answers OK.
- **If behind:** one round / half of Talk Time only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner says *I can ___.* or *I can't ___.*
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 line 1 together. Point to each instruction.
:::

:::step Real-World Reading · 7:15–7:25 · Job Application · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Real-World Reading page.
- **See it (2 min):** Task A — point to Name / Phone / Skills on the form.
- **Hear it (2 min):** spell a short name; learners write letters they know.
- **Read it (4 min):** Tasks B–C — check skills; write 2 *I can…* sentences.
- **Partners:** A asks one application question; B answers.
- **Learners who can't read yet:** they point to pictures and say key words with everyone.
- **If behind:** Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the items in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** match and write on their own. Help with instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line; learners check one face.
- **Collect if you want.** These checks are light practice, not a test grade.
- **If behind:** do it next week at 6:00.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L13_listening.mp3` (about 1:10; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Invented restaurant interview. Manager voice = Sam (am_michael).

> **Narrator:** Lesson thirteen. Listening. Ana has a job interview at a restaurant. Listen.
> **Manager:** Hi, Ana. Thanks for coming. Can you cook?
> **Ana:** Yes, I can. I can cook rice, beans, and tacos.
> **Manager:** Good. Can you work on weekends?
> **Ana:** Yes, I can. I can work on Saturday and Sunday.
> **Manager:** Can you work at night, too?
> **Ana:** Yes, I can. I don't work in the morning.
> **Manager:** Can you clean tables, too?
> **Ana:** Yes, I can. I can clean and I can cook.
> **Manager:** Can you use a computer?
> **Ana:** No, I can't. But I can learn.
> **Manager:** That's OK. Can you start on Monday?
> **Ana:** Yes, I can. Thank you so much!
> **Manager:** You're welcome. See you Monday at nine.
> **Ana:** See you Monday. Bye!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

**Listening A:** cook ✓; clean tables ✓; weekends ✓; night ✓; computer ✗ (but can learn); start Monday ✓. **B:** use a computer. **C:** Saturday, Sunday; Monday at nine.
**Grammar practice:** 1 can  2 can't  3 Can  4 can  5 can't  6–7 answers will vary
**Real-World Reading A:** 1 Name (the Name line)  2 Phone (the Phone line). **B:** answers will vary (skills check). **C:** answers will vary (*I can…* / *I can't…*). **D:** partner practice (no written key).
**Progress Check A** (teacher reads twice): 1 cook  2 drive  3 computer  4 clean  5 swim
**B:** 1–b  2–a  3–c  4–d
**C:** 1 can  2 can't  3 Can
**Scoring:** A /5 + B /4 + C /3 = /12. Not a test grade.

### Grammar Box 1: *Can you…?*

<!-- subst -->
| QUESTION | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Can** you | drive? / cook? / swim? | ‖ | Yes, | I **can**. |
| ^ | ^ | ‖ | No, | I **can't**. |

### Grammar Box 2: *I can… / I can't…*

<!-- subst -->
| SAY | < | ‖ | DON'T SAY | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I **can** | cook. / drive. | ‖ | I can | **to** cook. |
| I **can't** | use a computer. | ‖ | I can | cook**s**. |

### Grammar Box 3: Interview questions

<!-- subst -->
| MANAGER | < | ‖ | YOU | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Can you work | on weekends? | ‖ | Yes, I can. / No, I can't. | |
| Can you start | on Monday? | ‖ | Yes, | I can. |

---
### Questions and answers

**Q: Does *can* take *to* or *-s*?**
A: No. *I can drive.* (not *I can to drive* or *I can drives*).

**Q: How do *can* and *can't* sound?**
A: *can* is often short and soft. *can't* is longer and clear — stress it so people hear the *t*.


**Q: *can* + verb — with *to*?**
A: No. *I can cook* (not *I can to cook*).

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| I can to drive. | I can drive. |
| I can drives. | I can drive. |
| I no can cook. | I **can't** cook. |

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** Read the grammar boxes left to right. Copy one question and one answer.

*Nota para hispanohablantes: *can* no lleva *to* ni *-s*. *can* suena corto; *can't* suena claro y largo.*
