## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task. Learners read those on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Listen or write?"), **then go**. Say less, show more. Use the same short words every time: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the instruction and the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

## Lesson 1 (Week 1): Hello! What's Your Name?

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- say hello and goodbye (*Hello. Hi. Goodbye. See you next week.*)
- say their first and last name (*My name is Ana. I'm Ana. My last name is Ruiz.*)
- ask and answer *What's your name? How do you spell it?* and spell their name aloud
- ask for help: *Can you repeat, please?*

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Welcome + Hello Circle · 10 min
- **Grouping:** two circles (15 learners) / one circle (7–8 learners). Stand.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **Hello! My name is ___ .** Each learner writes a first name on a name tag.
- **Model:** wave and say, "**Hello! My name is** (your name)." Point to yourself. Then point to a learner and wait. Help with "My name is…".
- **Do:** around the circle, each person says "Hello! My name is ___." The group answers "Hello, ___!"
- **Round 2:** the same with "**I'm** ___." Point to the board: *My name is = I'm*.
- **Late arrivals:** join a circle. Give them a name tag.
- **If behind:** go around once only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:15 · Orientation: How Our Class Works · 5 min
- **Grouping:** whole class. Open the Student Pages to **How Our Class Works**.
- **Show the times:** point to the clock and the page: class 6:00, break 6:45, back 7:00, end 7:30.
- **Show the instruction words:** say each word and do the action: (hand to ear) "Listen." "Say." (mime writing) "Write." (point to two people) "Partner." Learners copy your gesture.
- **Say:** "Write your language here," and point to a "My language" line. Learners can fill it in now or at home. **No one has to.**
- **The 3 rules (say and show):** (1) "**English in class.** A classmate can help you for a few seconds. Then back to English." (2) "**Homework every week.** It's short." (3) "**Phones are OK** for the audio and a dictionary."
- **Also say:** "You can use a real name or a practice name." (See Teacher notes.)
- **If behind:** say the 3 rules only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:15–6:25 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** the New Words page. Hold up a real book and a real pen first.
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. Say each word slowly and clearly. Act out *hello* (wave), *goodbye* (wave and walk to the door), *spell* (write A-N-A in the air).
- **first name / last name:** write your own name on the board. Point to the first part: "**First name.**" Point to the second part: "**Last name.**" Do the same with 2 learners' names.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then switch. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs.
- **Classroom phrases (2 min):** turn to the Classroom Phrases page. Say each phrase with its gesture; learners repeat. Practice "**Can you repeat, please?**" right away: say a word too fast, and wait for someone to ask.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only. The other words stay on the page for home study.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:25–6:35 · Listening + "Say it" · 10 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** point to the pictures of Ana and Carlos. Say: "This is Ana. This is Carlos. First night. They say their names. They **spell** their names."
- **Listen 1 → Task A** (check Ana or Carlos). Pause after Ana spells her name, and ask the class to point: "Ana or Carlos?"
- **Listen 2 → Task B** (circle the letters). **Listen 3 → Task C** (write the two last names).
- **Pairs compare** after each task: "Show your partner." Then show the answers on the board.
- **"Say it" (2 min):** play or read 2 lines: "**How do you spell it?**" and "**Can you repeat, please?**" The class repeats each line twice. Use a flat, clear voice; then make it friendly.
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play the audio 2 times, and do Task C together on the board.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 6:35–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Q&A:** you read a question from the left side. The class reads an answer from the right side. Do Box 1 and Box 2 like this, then Box 3.
- **Say and write on the board:** "**What's = What is.** **I'm = I am.**"
- **Box 4 (2 min, a first look):** "Are you a student?" Class: "Yes, I am." Point to yourself: "Am I a student?" Class: "No, you're not. You're a teacher!" Don't teach more; Lesson 2 does.
- **Check questions:** "My name ___ Ana." (*is*) • "How do you ___ it?" (*spell*) • "Are you a teacher?" (*No, I'm not.*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A asks from the left side; B answers from the right side with real information (or a practice name). Then switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same questions.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Put the sign-in sheets on the tables. Write on the board: **What's your name? How do you spell it? Can you repeat, please?**
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time: Name Mingle with the Sign-In Sheet · 10 min
- **Grouping:** mingle. 15 learners: talk to 5–6 people. 7–8 learners: talk to everyone.
- **Model with one learner:** "Hello! What's your name?" → "Rosa." "How do you spell it?" → "R-O-S-A." Write R-O-S-A on the board **as you hear it**. Then show your paper to the learner: "Is this right?" → "Yes!"
- **Do:** learners stand, greet, ask, and **write the first and last name** on the sign-in sheet as the partner spells it. The partner looks and checks ✓ the spelling. Then they say "Goodbye!" and find a new person.
- **Help phrases:** point to the board when someone is stuck: "Can you repeat, please?"
- **Baseline snapshot:** while learners mingle, the teacher (or a second volunteer) calls about 5 learners, one at a time, for 2 minutes and uses the checklist in the answer key. Do the rest in Weeks 2 and 3.
- **With 5 or fewer learners:** sit at one table and go around; everyone writes everyone's name.
- **If behind:** learners talk to 3 people.
:::

:::step Step 7 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner **says** one sentence to you at the door ("My name is ___. ___-___-___.") **or writes** one sentence on a slip (*My name is ___.*). Copying from the grammar box is fine.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together. Point to each instruction so learners know what to do at home.
:::

:::step Letters & Numbers · 7:15–7:25 · The Alphabet · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Letters & Numbers page and the alphabet on the board.
- **See it (2 min):** point to each letter on the board, A to Z. Say each letter once. Point to a capital and its small letter: "Big A, small a."
- **Say it (4 min):** learners repeat A to Z after you, in groups of 4–5 letters (A B C D, E F G, H I J K…). Then go to the **tricky letters** box. Say each letter 3 times, slowly, with its English example word. Spend the most time on **A / E / I** and **G / J**.
- **Quick check (1 min):** say a tricky letter; learners point to it on their page. Say: "**E**." (They point to E, not I.) "**G**." (G, not J.)
- **Write it (3 min):** Task B: say the 5 practice letters (the list is in the answer key). Learners circle them. Then Task C: learners write their name in capital letters and small letters.
- **Partners:** Task D: A spells their name; B writes it on a whiteboard or paper. A checks. Switch.
- **Learners who can't read or write yet:** they say the letters with everyone, point instead of circle, and copy their name from their name tag. A helper, if you have one, sits with them.
- **If behind:** do the tricky letters and Task D only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · First can-do self-check · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 5 letters (answer key), slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** learners match and write on their own. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "A little." 😐 "Not yet." Learners check one.
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 2). It shows who needs more help with letters.
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 2 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Answer key

- *Listening Task A:* 1 Ana, 2 Carlos, 3 Carlos, 4 Carlos.
- *Listening Task B:* Ana: R **U** **I** **Z** • Carlos: **V** **E** **G** A.
- *Listening Task C:* RUIZ (Ana) • VEGA (Carlos).
- *Grammar practice (Write the missing word):* 1 name, 2 What's, 3 spell, 4 repeat, 5 am.
- *Homework 3 (write the word):* 1 book, 2 pen, 3 teacher, 4 student.
- *Letters & Numbers Task B (the teacher says):* 1 **E**, 2 **G**, 3 **I**, 4 **J**, 5 **A**. Say each letter 2 times.
- *Progress Check Part A (the teacher says):* 1 **I**, 2 **J**, 3 **E**, 4 **H**, 5 **G**. Say each letter 2 times.
- *Progress Check Part B:* 1 c, 2 d, 3 a, 4 b.
- *Progress Check scoring:* Part A /5 + Part B /4 + Part C /2 = /11. This is not a test grade. It shows who needs help with letters (Part A) and with reading the questions (Part B).

### Teacher notes

- **Names and privacy:** every learner may use a **practice name** in class (for example, a name from the listening). Never ask for immigration status, money, or a real address. Practice phone numbers start with 555-01 (for example, 555-0142).
- **Spelling names:** write each name on the board **exactly as you hear it**. If you write the wrong letter, that is useful: the class sees which letter names are not clear yet. Smile, ask "Can you repeat, please?", and fix it together.
- **The letters learners mix up most:** **A / E / I** (in English, *E* sounds like "ee" and *I* sounds like "eye"), **G / J**, **H** ("aych"), **Y** ("why"), **W** ("double-u"), and **V / B**. When you say these, say them slowly, and point to the letter.
- **First name and last name:** in some cultures the family name comes first, and some learners have two last names. Say: "In the U.S., forms want the **first name** first." Two last names are fine. Learners write both, or the one they use on forms.
- **Mixed levels:** learners who already know the alphabet spell longer names (a city, a street) in Talk Time and help a partner. New learners use the grammar box as a script.
- **Learners who can't read or write yet:** they take part fully in all the speaking. In reading and writing tasks, they point instead of circle, and copy from the board or their name tag. Don't make them read aloud.
- **Typical mistakes this week:** *My name Ana* (no *is*), *I Ana* (no *'m*), and saying the Spanish names of the letters (*E* said as "eh," *I* said as "ee"). Correct by saying the right form clearly; the learner repeats. Learners can read the note on their Grammar page themselves; you don't need to explain it.

### Questions and answers

**Q: What is *What's*?**

A: *What's* = *What **is***. *I'm* = *I **am***.

**Q: How do I say my name?**

A: *My name **is** Ana.* or *I'**m** Ana.* Both are good.

**Q: First name or last name?**

A: *Ana* = first name. *Ruiz* = last name (family name). In English, the first name comes first: *Ana Ruiz*.

**Q: How do I spell my name?**

A: Say each letter in English, with a short stop: *R… U… I… Z.*

**Q: I don't understand. What do I say?**

A: *Can you repeat, please?* or *I don't understand.*

5. Are you a student? Yes, I ____________.

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| My name Ana. | My name **is** Ana. |
| I Ana. | **I'm** Ana. |
| What your name? | What**'s** your name? |
| How you spell it? | How **do you** spell it? |
| Yes, I'm. | Yes, I **am**. |
| Repeat, please! (too strong) | **Can you** repeat, please? |

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** En inglés, varias letras tienen otro nombre: **A** se dice «ei», **E** se dice «i», **I** se dice «ai», **G** se dice «yi», **J** se dice «yei», **H** se dice «eich», **Y** se dice «uai» y **W** se dice «dábel-iu». *What's* = *What is* (¿Cuál es…?). Di *My name is Ana* o *I'm Ana* (no «I call me Ana»). Siempre usa *is* o *'m*: no «My name Ana».

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** In English, the first name comes first and the family name comes last: *Ana Ruiz*. Always use *is* or *'m* in these answers: *My name **is** Ana. I'**m** Ana.*
