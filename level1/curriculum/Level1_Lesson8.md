**Status:** Draft for review (expanded to match Lessons 5–7 depth).

## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task. Learners read those on their own.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Listen or write?"), **then go**. Say less, show more. Use the same short words every time: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the instruction and the grammar box; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English.

> **Food tip:** Ask about allergies and food rules without making anyone explain why. *"I don't eat ___."* is always an OK answer. Never ask how much learners spend on food.

## Lesson 8 (Week 9): Let's Eat!

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- name common foods and drinks (breakfast, lunch, dinner, beans, tortillas, meat, fish, fruit, vegetables, juice, soda, pizza)

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **How much is this shirt? Do you like coffee? Do you work on weekends?**
- **Model with one learner:** "Do you like coffee?" → "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't."
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show Lesson 7 homework (prices). Walk around and look.
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner. They can review Lesson 7 New Words at home.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** hold up or draw a taco: "This is a **taco**. **Tortillas**. **Beans**." Mime eating: "**Breakfast**. **Lunch**. **Dinner**."
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. ★ words twice more.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then A asks "Do you like ___?" and B answers *Yes, I do. / No, I don't.*
- **Spanish tip (on the page only):** *I like tacos* (no *the* for things in general).
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Ana is at a **taco truck**. She asks: **Do you like…?** She **wants** food." Point to Task A pictures.
- **Listen 1 → Task A:** circle what Ana orders.
- **Listen 2 → Task B:** does the server like fish tacos? Circle 😀 or 😐.
- **Listen 3 → Task C:** write the prices, cash, change, for here / to go.
- **Pairs compare** after each task. Write answers on the board.
- **Say it:** class repeats *I want a fish taco and a soda, please.* and *For here or to go?*
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play 2 times; do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** Grammar page. **Left = question. Right = answer.**
- **Boxes 1–2:** "Do you like fish?" → "Yes, I do. / No, I don't." "I like tacos. I don't like coffee." Cross out *Me like* and *I like the tacos* (for things in general).
- **Box 3:** "What do you want?" → "I want a taco." / "A coffee, please."
- **Box 4:** mime the counter: "For here or to go?" → "To go, please." "Anything else?" → "No, thanks."
- **Check questions:** "Do you ___ fish?" (*like*) • "I ___ a soda." (*want*) • "To go or ___ here?" (*for*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A asks *Do you like…?* B answers; then A orders from the menu and B is the server.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same language.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Put **Student A** (server) and **Student B** (customer) pages in two piles. Write on the board: **Do you like ___? I want ___. For here or to go? How much is it?**
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time: Food Survey + Taco Truck · 10 min
- **Grouping:** groups of 3–4 for the survey; then A–B pairs for the truck. Partners **don't show** their role cards if you cut them apart.
- **Part 1 — Food survey (4 min):** each learner asks 3 classmates *Do you like…?* and writes 😀 or 😐. Model: "Do you like fish?" → "Yes, I do." (write 😀).
- **Part 2 — Taco truck (5 min):** **A is the server** (menu with prices). **B is the customer**: orders, says for here / to go, asks the price, pays. Then **switch**.
- **Model with one learner** before Part 2.
- **If behind:** survey only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner **says** *I like ___.* or *I want ___.* **or writes** one sentence. Copying from the box is fine.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 line 1 together. Point to each instruction.
:::

:::step Real-World Reading · 7:15–7:25 · Picture Menu · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Real-World Reading page (same menu as Talk Time).
- **See it (2 min):** Task A — point to each food and the price. Say the prices as words.
- **Hear it (2 min):** say 3 prices; learners point.
- **Read it (4 min):** Tasks B–C — *for here / to go*; copy one menu line.
- **Partners:** A is the server, B orders one thing from the menu (no money this time).
- **Learners who can't read yet:** they point to pictures and say the food words with everyone.
- **If behind:** Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals.
- **Part A:** read the 3 words (answer key), slowly, 2 times. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** match and write. Help with instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line; learners check one face.
- **Collect if you want.** After the midterm, these checks are light.
- **If behind:** do it next week at 6:00.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L8_listening.mp3` (about 1:10; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Say the prices as words: *three dollars, two dollars, five dollars, ten dollars.*

> **Narrator:** Lesson eight. Listening. Ana is at a taco truck. She talks to the server about food she likes. Listen.
> **Server:** Hi! What do you want today?
> **Ana:** Hi. Do you like the fish tacos?
> **Server:** Yes, I do. They're really good.
> **Ana:** Do you like the bean tacos, too?
> **Server:** Yes, I do. A lot of people like them.
> **Ana:** OK. I want a fish taco and a soda, please.
> **Server:** For here or to go?
> **Ana:** To go, please.
> **Server:** Anything else? Juice or fruit?
> **Ana:** No, thanks. How much is it?
> **Server:** The taco is three dollars. The soda is two dollars. That's five dollars.
> **Ana:** OK. Here's ten dollars.
> **Server:** Thank you. Your change is five dollars. Have a nice day!
> **Ana:** You too. Bye!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

**Listening**
- **A:** fish taco, soda (not pizza, not juice)
- **B:** 😀 likes
- **C:** taco $3; soda $2; total $5; cash $10; change $5; **to go**

**Grammar practice:** 1 like  2 don't  3 want  4 to go  5 like  6 like or want (answers will vary)

**Homework 2:** 1 I like fruit.  2 I don't like coffee.  3 I want a taco.

**Real-World Reading A:** taco $3; juice $2; pizza slice $3; soda $1. **B:** 1 for here  2 to go  3 Anything else?
**C:** answers will vary (copy from menu). **D:** partner practice (no written key).

**Progress Check A** (teacher reads twice each): 1 breakfast  2 fish  3 juice  4 beans  5 lunch
**B:** 1–b  2–c  3–a  4–d
**C:** answers vary (like / don't like / want)
**Scoring:** A /5 + B /4 + C /3 = /12. Not a test grade.

### Teacher notes

- Invented prices and a fictional taco truck. No real restaurant names.
- After the midterm (Week 8), keep energy light; this lesson recycles *Do you…?* and prices from Lesson 7.
- Allergies: accept *I don't eat ___* or *I don't like ___* with no follow-up questions about why.
- *I'd like*, *some / any*, and count vs non-count stay in Level 2.
- **Big mistake this week:** *Me like tacos* and *I like the tacos* (for food in general). Point to Grammar Box 2.
- Learners who can't write yet: they can point on the menu and say *A taco, please* / *To go*.
- Talk Time odd number: two customers share one server, or one survey trio.

| QUESTION | < | ‖ | ANSWER | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Do** you | like fish? / like tacos? / like coffee? | ‖ | Yes, | I **do**. |

### Grammar Box 2: *I like… / I don't like…*

<!-- subst -->
| SAY | < | ‖ | DON'T SAY | < |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I **like** | tacos. / fruit. / juice. | ‖ | I like | **the** tacos. (for things in general) |

### Questions and answers

**Q: *like* or *want*?**
A: *like* = it's good for me (*I like fish*). *want* = I ask for it now (*I want a taco*).

**Q: *do* or *don't*?**
A: *Yes, I do.* / *No, I don't.* After *I*, use *don't* + verb: *I don't like coffee.*

**Q: *Anything else?***
A: The server asks if you want more. Answer *No, thanks.* or name one more food.

**Q: Do I need *a* / *an*?**
A: *I want **a** taco. I want **an** apple.* (one thing)

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| Me like tacos. | **I like** tacos. |
| I like the tacos. (in general) | I like tacos. |
| I no like coffee. | I **don't** like coffee. |
| I want taco. | I want **a** taco. |

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** *like* = general preference. *want* = order now. Read Grammar Boxes 1–4 left to right.

*Nota para hispanohablantes: En inglés, *like* no funciona como *gustar*. Di **I like tacos** (no «Me like» ni «I like the tacos» para las cosas en general).*
