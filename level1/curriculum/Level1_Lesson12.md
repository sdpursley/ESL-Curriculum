## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it**, **check it**, **then go**. Same short words: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* neighbor (odd → one group of 3). *Groups:* 3–4. *Mingle:* stand and talk to many classmates.

> **When a learner is stuck:** show the picture or board → one short English example → point to the grammar box → classmate helps briefly, then English again.

> **Practice only.** The 911 dialogue is not a real emergency. Never ask learners to share real medical details.

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **Where's the pharmacy? How do you feel? Are you OK?**
- **Model with one learner:** "Where's the pharmacy?" → "It's next to the bank." Then "How do you feel?" → "I feel sick."
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show last week's homework. Walk around and look.
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** point to body pictures: "**Head**. **Stomach**. **Arm**. **Leg**." Mime: "**I feel sick**. **It hurts here**."
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. ★ words twice more.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then A asks *Are you OK?* B answers *No, I'm not. I feel sick.*
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "Part one: Rosa and Marco are at **work**. Part two: **practice only** — a call to **9 1 1**. Not a real emergency."
- **Listen 1 → Task A.** **Listen 2 → Task B.** **Listen 3 → Task C.**
- **Pairs compare** after each task. Write answers on the board.
- **Say it:** class repeats: *I feel sick.* and *Where does it hurt?*
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play 2 times; do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** Grammar page. **Left = question. Right = answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Box 1:** *How do you feel? → I feel sick. / I'm tired.*
- **Box 2:** *Are you OK? → No, I'm not. I'm sick. / I'm hurt.*
- **Box 3:** *What's wrong? Where does it hurt? → Here.* (point)
- **Box 4:** Commands: *Call 911. Sit down. Go to the doctor. I need help.*
- **Check:** "I ___ sick." (*feel* / *am*) • "Call ___" (*911*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A mimes a body part; B asks *Where does it hurt?* A points: *Here. My ___.*
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. Talk Time practices the same language.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **How do you feel? Are you OK? Where does it hurt? Call 911.** Remind: Part 2 of the audio is **practice only**.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs, then groups of 3 for the practice call.
- **Part 1 (4 min):** A mimes sick/hurt; B asks *Are you OK? What's wrong? Where does it hurt?*
- **Part 2 (5 min):** practice 911 role play from the card (**not a real call**). Switch roles.
- **If behind:** one round / half of Talk Time only.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner says *I feel sick.* or *It hurts here.* or *Call 911.*
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 line 1 together. Point to each instruction.
:::

:::step Real-World Reading · 7:15–7:25 · Medicine Label + Emergency Card · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Real-World Reading page.
- **See it (2 min):** Task A — point to *Every 6 hours* and *With water* on the medicine label.
- **Hear it (2 min):** say *Every 6 hours* / *With water*; learners point.
- **Read it (4 min):** Tasks B–C — emergency card name/phone; what to do.
- **Partners:** A reads one label line; B repeats.
- **Learners who can't read yet:** they point to pictures and say key words with everyone.
- **If behind:** Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the items in the answer key, slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** match and write on their own. Help with instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line; learners check one face.
- **Collect if you want.** These checks are light practice, not a test grade.
- **If behind:** do it next week at 6:00.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L12_listening.mp3` (about 1:10; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Part two is practice only — say that before you play. Not a real emergency.

> **Narrator:** Lesson twelve. Listening. Part one. Rosa and Marco are at work. Listen.
> **Rosa:** Marco, are you OK?
> **Marco:** No, I'm not. I feel sick.
> **Rosa:** What's wrong?
> **Marco:** It hurts here. My stomach.
> **Rosa:** Sit down. Do you need water?
> **Marco:** Yes, please. Then I want to go home.
> **Rosa:** Do you need a doctor?
> **Marco:** Yes. I need a doctor.
> **Rosa:** OK. Go home. Call the doctor.
> **Narrator:** Part two. Practice only. This is not a real emergency. A caller talks to 9 1 1. Listen.
> **Operator:** 9 1 1. What's your emergency?
> **Ana:** I need help. I'm hurt.
> **Operator:** What's wrong? Where does it hurt?
> **Ana:** It hurts here. My arm.
> **Operator:** What's the address?
> **Ana:** Two ten Pine Street.
> **Operator:** OK. Help is coming. Stay on the phone.
> **Ana:** Thank you.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

**Listening A:** 1 sick  2 stomach. **B:** 210 (Pine Street). **C:** Sit down; Go home; Call the doctor (not Call 911).
**Grammar practice:** 1 feel  2 OK  3 hurts  4 Call  5 feel  6 answers will vary
**Real-World Reading A:** 1 Every 6 hours  2 water. **B:** Name ANA RUIZ; Phone 555-0142. **C:** Call 911; Sit down; Go to the doctor (not Run away from help). **D:** partner practice (no written key).
**Progress Check A** (teacher reads twice): 1 sick  2 head  3 doctor  4 arm  5 stomach
**B:** 1–b  2–a  3–c  4–d
**C:** 1 feel  2 hurts  3 Call
**Scoring:** A /5 + B /4 + C /3 = /12. Not a test grade.

### Teacher notes

- **Practice only** for 911. Invented address (210 Pine Street).
- Never force real medical stories. Pointing + *Here* is enough.
- *My ___ hurts* and symptom words stay mostly in Level 2; Level 1 uses *It hurts here* + body part.
- **Big mistake:** sharing private medical details in class — stop gently and return to the picture.
- Learners who can't write yet: they point to body pictures and say *Here.*

### Questions and answers

**Q: *I'm sick* or *I have sick*?**
A: *I'm sick. I'm tired. I'm cold.* (not *I have sick*).

**Q: Do we say *My head hurts* in Level 1?**
A: You may hear it. In this lesson, say *It hurts here* and point. Level 2 practices *My ___ hurts*.


**Q: Do I say real medical problems in class?**
A: No. Use the pictures. Point and say *Here.*

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| I have sick. | I'm **sick**. |
| I have hungry. | I'm hungry. |
| Call to 911. | Call **911**. |

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** Read the grammar boxes left to right. Copy one question and one answer.

*Nota para hispanohablantes: *I'm sick / I'm tired / I'm cold* (no «I have…»). *My head hurts* = *Me duele la cabeza* (Level 2 lo practica).*
