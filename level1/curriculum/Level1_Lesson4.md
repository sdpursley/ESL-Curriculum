**Audience:** Program coordinators and volunteer teachers

## How to Use This Plan

> **You don't need to speak Spanish or any other language.** Teach in English. Show, point, and gesture. The student pages have a Spanish line and a "My language" line for every task. Learners read those on their own. You never need to read, translate, or explain them.

> **Instruction rule:** **Model it** (do it with one learner while everyone watches), **check it** (ask: "Listen or write?"), **then go**. Say less, show more. Use the same short words every time: *Listen. Repeat. Point. Circle. Write. Partner. Group.*

> **Grouping toolkit:** *Pairs:* turn to a neighbor; with an odd number, make one group of 3. *Groups:* 3–4 learners. *Mingle:* everyone stands and talks to many classmates. The teacher joins a pair only if one learner is left over.

> **When a learner is stuck or speaks another language to you:** (1) show it: the picture, a gesture, or the board; (2) give one more short English example; (3) point to the instruction and the grammar box on their page; (4) a classmate can help for a few seconds, then back to English. You don't need to understand what classmates say to each other.

> **Family can be a sensitive topic.** Some learners have family far away, have lost family members, or don't want to talk about their family. **Every activity works with the picture-card family (Ana's family)**, and you say this at the start: "You can talk about your family **or** Ana's family." *Married / single* is **optional**: never ask a learner directly if they are married.

## Lesson 4 (Week 4): This Is My Family

### At a glance

:::box Objectives: learners can…
- name family members (*mother, father, son, daughter, brother, sister…*)
- say *This is my brother. His name is Luis. He's 25.*
- ask and answer *Who's this?* and *How old is he / she?*
- ask and answer *Do you have kids? Yes, I do. I have two. / No, I don't.*
- use *'s* (*Ana's husband, Carlos's son*)
- hear and say short vowels (*dad, mom, son, kids*) and write plural *-s* (Letters & Numbers block)
:::

:::box Materials
- Student Pages for Lesson 4 (print for the number expected + 3 spares)

- phone + small speaker, `L1_L4_listening.mp3`
- **Prep time:** 30 min.
:::

### Step by step

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Hello + Review · 10 min
- **Grouping:** pairs. 15 learners: 7 pairs + 1 group of 3. 7–8 learners: 3–4 pairs. Late arrivals join any pair.
- **Before class:** write on the board: **What's your phone number? What's your address? What's his / her name?**
- **Model with one learner:** "What's your phone number?" → "555-0163." "Can you repeat, please?" Then point to another learner: "What's **her** name?" → "**Her** name is Rosa."
- **Do:** pairs use the **Hello + Review** page. Practice numbers (555-01__) are fine. Then they ask about a classmate (*What's his name? Where is she from?*). Switch. Then find a new partner.
- **Homework check (last 3 min):** partners show each other their Lesson 3 homework. Walk around and look at item 2 (*my / his / her / our / their*).
- **New learners:** pair them with a strong partner. They can do the Lesson 1–3 Progress Checks at home.
- **If behind:** one partner only.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:20 · New Words · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Say first:** "Today: **family**. You can talk about **your** family **or** Ana's family." Point to the picture-card family page.
- **Show:** draw a simple family tree on the board (stick figures are fine), or show one photo of a family. Point and say: "This is the **mother**. This is the **father**. They are the **parents**."
- **Do:** point to each picture and say the word. Learners repeat twice. The person with the **blue circle** is the word. For *husband / wife*, point to the ring.
- ***kids* = *children*:** write **kids = children** on the board. "Kids" is everyday English. Also write **1 child → 2 children**.
- **Pairs (4 min):** A points to a picture, B says the word. Then switch. Then A says a word, B points.
- **If behind:** teach the ★ words only.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:20–6:32 · Listening · 12 min
- **Grouping:** individuals, then pairs check.
- **Before you listen (1 min):** say: "This is Carlos. This is Kim. Carlos has **photos of his family** on his phone." Hold up your phone and pretend to show photos: "This is my wife… This is my son…"
- **Listen 1 → Task A:** learners **point** to each person they hear, then write 1, 2, 3, 4 under the pictures in the order they hear them.
- **Listen 2 → Task B:** learners write the names on Carlos's family tree (the names are in the box).
- **Listen 3 → Task C:** learners write the numbers (how many children, ages).
- **Pairs compare** after each task: "Show your partner." Then write the answers on the board.
- **The audio plays the conversation once.** Press play again for the next listen.
- **If behind:** play the audio 2 times, and do Task C together.
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:32–6:45 · Grammar Q&A · 13 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs.
- **Show:** the Grammar page. The **left side is the question. The right side is the answer.** Point left, then right.
- **Box 1:** point to the picture-card family. "Who's this?" → "This is my mother. **Her** name is Lucia." A man → **his**. A woman → **her**. (The same as Lesson 3.)
- **Box 2 (the key point):** "Do you have kids?" Nod: "Yes, I **do**." Shake your head: "No, I **don't**." Write on the board: **Yes, I do.** ✓ / **Yes, I have.** ✗. Then add the number: "Yes, I do. I have two."
- **Box 3:** hold up a learner's pen: "This is **Maria's** pen." Then: "Ana's husband" = the husband of Ana. Say *Carlos's* = "CAR-los-iz".
- **Box 4:** one son → two son**s**. Clap on the *-s*. Point to **children** and **babies**: these are different.
- **Check questions:** "Do you have kids?" (Yes, I do. / No, I don't.) • point to a boy in a picture: "___ name is Daniel." (*His*) • "Ana has a husband. He's ___ husband." (*Ana's*)
- **Pair drill (last 3–4 min):** A asks from the left side about the picture-card family (*Who's this? How old is he?*); B answers from the right side. Then switch.
- **If behind:** skip the pair drill. It becomes homework, and Talk Time practices the same questions.
:::

:::step Break · 6:45–7:00 · 15 min
- Write on the board: **This is my ___. His / Her name is ___. He's / She's ___.** and **Who's this? How old is he / she? Do you have kids?**
- Draw the blank family tree on the board.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:10 · Talk Time: My Family Tree · 10 min
- **Grouping:** groups of 3. 15 learners: 5 groups. 7–8 learners: 2 groups of 3 + 1 pair (or 2 groups of 4).
- **Say again:** "Your family **or** Ana's family. You choose." Don't ask who chooses what.
- **Model (2 min):** fill in the board tree with your family or an invented family. Say 3 sentences: "This is my sister. Her name is Laura. She's 30." Then a learner asks you one question: "How old is he?"
- **Part 1 (3 min):** each learner fills in the blank tree with names (and ages if they want), **or** uses the picture-card family page. Learners who can't write yet can draw faces or point to the pictures.
- **Part 2 (5 min):** in the group, each person shows the tree and says **3 sentences**. The others ask **one question** each (*Who's this? How old is he? Do you have kids?*).
- **Fast groups:** they say one sentence about a partner's family with *'s*: "**Rosa's** son is 12."
- **If behind:** each person says 2 sentences, and skip the questions.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:10–7:15 · Exit Ticket + Homework · 5 min · NEVER SKIP
- **Exit ticket:** each learner **says** one sentence about a family member (or a person in Ana's family): "This is my son. His name is…" **or writes** *Yes, I do.* / *No, I don't.* for "Do you have kids?". Copying is fine.
- **Homework:** open the Homework page. Do item 1 together (the first sentence). Point to each instruction so learners know what to do at home.
:::

:::step Letters & Numbers · 7:15–7:25 · Short Vowels, Plural -s, This / Is / My · 10 min
- **Grouping:** whole class, then pairs. Use the Letters & Numbers page.
- **See it (1 min):** point to the 4 columns in Task A: **a** (*dad*), **o** (*mom*), **u sound** (*son*), **i** (*kids*).
- **Say it (3 min):** say each column, top to bottom. Learners repeat. The vowels are **short**: say them quickly. Point out: *son* and *mother* are spelled with **o**, but sound like *u* in *but*.
- **Quick check (1 min):** say *dad* or *kids*… Learners point to the column.
- **Write it (4 min):** Task B: say the 5 words in the answer key, 2 times each. Learners circle. Task C: one → two: learners add **-s**. Show *child → children* (no *-s*). Task D: learners copy the 2 sentences. Point to the capital **T** and the period.
- **Partners:** A points to a word in Task A, B reads it. Switch.
- **Learners who can't read or write yet:** they say the words with everyone and point. In Task D they copy one sentence. A helper, if you have one, sits with them.
- **If behind:** do Tasks A and B only.
:::

:::step Progress Check · 7:25–7:30 · 5 min
- **Grouping:** individuals. Open the Progress Check page.
- **Part A:** read the 5 words (answer key), slowly, 2 times each. Learners circle.
- **Parts B and C:** learners match and write on their own. Walk around and help with the instructions only.
- **Part D:** read each "I can…" line aloud and point to the 3 faces: 😀 "I can do it." 🙂 "A little." 😐 "Not yet." Learners check one.
- **Collect the pages** (or check them at the start of Week 5). Part C shows who still mixes up *his* and *her*.
- **If behind:** do the Progress Check in Week 5 at 6:00, while learners arrive.
:::

### Listening script: `L1_L4_listening.mp3` (about 1:15; the conversation plays once, so play the audio again if needed)

> **Audio:** play from a phone + small speaker. No speaker? Read the script with a volunteer. Hold up your phone at "Look."

> **Narrator:** Lesson four. Listening. Carlos and Kim are at English class. Carlos has photos of his family on his phone. Listen.
> **Kim:** Carlos, are those photos of your family?
> **Carlos:** Yes, they are. Look. This is my wife. Her name is Elena.
> **Kim:** Oh, nice. And who's this?
> **Carlos:** This is my son. His name is Daniel. He's ten.
> **Kim:** And this girl? Is she your daughter?
> **Carlos:** Yes, she is. This is my daughter, Sofia. She's six.
> **Kim:** Oh, and who's this? A baby!
> **Carlos:** That's our baby, Leo. He's one.
> **Kim:** So you have three children!
> **Carlos:** Yes, I do. I have three kids. And these are my parents, Carmen and Pedro.
> **Kim:** Your parents are here, too?
> **Carlos:** No. They're in Colombia. What about you, Kim? Do you have kids?
> **Kim:** No, I don't. But I have two brothers.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Listening Task A (order you hear):* a son **2**, b baby **4**, c wife **1**, d daughter **3**.
- *Listening Task B (Carlos's family tree):* mother **Carmen**, father **Pedro**, wife **Elena**, son **Daniel**, daughter **Sofia**, baby **Leo**.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 **3** (three children), 2 **10**, 3 **6**, 4 **0** (Kim: "No, I don't."), 5 **2** (two brothers).
- *Grammar practice:* 1 do, 2 don't, 3 Her, 4 His, 5 Carlos's.
- *Homework 2 (do / don't):* 1 do, 2 don't, 3 do, 4 don't.
- *Homework 3 ('s):* 1 Carlos's son, 2 Kim's brothers, 3 Rosa's daughter.
- *Letters & Numbers Task B (the teacher says):* 1 **mom**, 2 **dad**, 3 **kids**, 4 **his**, 5 **big**. Say each word 2 times.
- *Letters & Numbers Task C:* brothers, sisters, sons, daughters, parents (and *child → children*).
- *Progress Check Part A (the teacher says):* 1 **father**, 2 **daughter**, 3 **wife**, 4 **brother**, 5 **grandmother**. Say each word 2 times.
- *Progress Check Part B:* 1 c, 2 d, 3 a, 4 b.
- *Progress Check Part C:* 1 Her, 2 His, 3 Ana's.
- *Progress Check scoring:* Part A /5 + Part B /4 + Part C /3 = /12. This is not a test grade. Part A shows the family words; Part C shows *his / her* and *'s*.

### Teacher notes

- **Sensitive topic:** always offer the picture-card family. Never ask "Where is your husband?" or "Why don't you have kids?" Accept *No, I don't* and move on. *Married / single* is optional (one Q&A line on the Grammar page): don't ask it to a learner in front of the class.
- **The big mistake this week: *Yes, I have.*** Spanish answers *Sí, tengo*. In English the short answer uses *do*: **Yes, I do. / No, I don't.** Then learners can add the number: *I have two.* Correct by nodding and saying "Yes, I **do**."
- ***his / her* again:** many learners say *my sister… his name*. Point to the person in the picture: "A woman… **her**." Learners can read the Spanish note on their Grammar page; you don't need to explain it.
- ***'s*:** *Ana's husband* (not *the husband of Ana*). Names that end in *s* add *'s* too: *Carlos's* ("CAR-los-iz").
- ***parents* is not "relatives":** *parents* = mother and father only. For "all my family" say *my family* (or *my relatives*, for strong learners).
- **Plurals:** most words add *-s* (*brothers*); *child → children* and *baby → babies* are different. *Kids* is common and easy: use it.
- **Ages:** learners don't have to say their own age or their family's ages. Ana's family has ages for practice.
- **Mixed levels:** strong learners add *'s* and a question (*How old is Rosa's son?*). New learners use only *This is my ___.* and point.
- **Learners who can't read or write yet:** they take part fully in the speaking. On the tree they can draw faces instead of names. Don't make them read aloud.
- **Looking ahead:** Lesson 5 (jobs) uses *do* again (*Do you work at night?*) and *I don't…*, and previews *Does he / she…?*.

### Questions and answers

**Q: *Do you have kids?* Can I say "Yes, I have"?**

A: No. Say **Yes, I do.** or **No, I don't.** Then you can add the number: *Yes, I do. I have two.*

**Q: What are *parents*?**

A: Mother **and** father. Only 2 people. All the people in your family = *my family*.

**Q: I don't want to talk about my family. What do I do?**

A: Use **Ana's family** (the picture-card family). It's OK.

### Common mistakes

| ✗ Don't say | ✓ Say |
|---|---|
| Do you have kids? Yes, I have. | Yes, I **do**. (I have two.) |
| You have kids? | **Do** you have kids? |
| the husband of Ana | **Ana's** husband |
| This is my sister. His name is Ana. | **Her** name is Ana. |
| I have two son. | I have two son**s**. |
| I have many parents in Mexico. | I have a big **family** in Mexico. |

**Optional. Q: *Are you married?***

A: *Yes, I am.* / *No, I'm not.* You don't have to answer this question.

> **Nota para hispanohablantes:** En inglés se dice *Ana's husband* (no «the husband of Ana»). Para preguntar se usa *do*: *Do you have kids?* La respuesta corta es *Yes, I do.* o *No, I don't.* (no «Yes, I have»). Se dice *I have two kids* (no «I have two kids of me»). *Parents* significa solo padre y madre; no significa «parientes».

> **Tip for speakers of other languages:** Questions with *have* start with *Do*: *Do you have kids?* Answer *Yes, I do.* or *No, I don't.* *Ana's husband* = the husband of Ana. *Parents* = mother and father.
