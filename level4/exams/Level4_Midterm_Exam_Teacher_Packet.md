## How to Use This Packet

- **The test for learners is a separate file:** `Level4_Midterm_Student_Test.pdf` (no answers, no cover page). Print 1 per learner + 3 extra.
- **This packet** is for you: directions, the listening script, the answer key, the scoring guide, and the speaking papers.
- **Audio:** the recording `L4_Midterm_listening.mp3` is not ready yet (the script is about 1:57 long). Until it is ready, read the listening script aloud two times, at a natural speed. Two readers are better: one reads the women's lines and one reads the men's lines.
- **All instructions are in English.** Read each instruction aloud once and show an example on the board if learners look unsure.

## Week 8: Midterm: Step by Step

### What the test has

| Part | What learners do | Points | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Listening | Listen to two conversations (a clinic check-in; a favor between neighbors). Circle an answer, mark true or false, or write one word (10 items). | 10 | 15 min (6:10–6:25) |
| B. Words | Complete sentences with words from a box (8 items). | 8 | 20 min (6:25–6:45) for B + C |
| C. Grammar | Part 1: circle the correct words (15 items). Part 2: find and fix one mistake in each sentence (5 items). | 20 | (with B) |
| D. Reading | Read a text of about 163 words. Answer 6 questions. | 6 | 12 min (7:00–7:12) |
| E. Writing | Choose one of two tasks. Write 5–8 sentences. | 6 | 15 min (7:12–7:27) |
| **Written total** | | **50** | **60 min** |
| Speaking interview | Pairs with the teacher, during the written parts. Scored with the rubric, not added to the written total. | 3–9 | 4 min per pair |

*Covers Lessons 1–7. Listening: a clinic check-in (Lesson 4, with ever/never, for/since, already/yet) and a favor between neighbors (Lesson 5). Grammar: present perfect with ever/never, for/since, already/yet; present perfect or simple past; suggestions; Would you mind + -ing; If I had…, I'd…; too/enough, much/many; must/don't have to. Reading: an update email to a supervisor. Writing: an update email or an email that asks a favor.*

### Before class (20 minutes)

1. Print the student test (1 per learner + 3). From this packet, print the Speaking Task Cards (1 set per pair), the Score Sheet, and the Self-Check (1 per learner).
2. The recording is not ready yet: ask a second reader to help, and read the listening script through once before class.
3. Set 2 chairs and 1 chair for you in a quiet corner (for the speaking interviews).
4. Have pencils, a clock or timer, and the class list.

:::step Step 1 · 6:00–6:10 · Welcome and warm-up · 10 min
- **Say:** "Tonight is the test. First listening, then words and grammar, then a break, then reading and writing. I will call you in pairs for a short speaking interview."
- Warm-up: pairs ask each other 2 questions from the board (Have you ever…? How long have you…?) and answer in 3 sentences. Call the first pair for the interview.
:::

:::step Step 2 · 6:10–6:25 · A. Listening · 15 min
- Hand out the test. Learners write their name. Read the Part A instruction and the scene line aloud.
- Give 1 minute to read the questions. Play the audio (or read the script) **once**. Wait 1 minute. Play it **again**. Give 2 more minutes.
- No interviews during the listening.
:::

:::step Step 3 · 6:25–6:45 · B. Words and C. Grammar · 20 min
- Read the Part B and Part C instructions aloud. Show Part C2 on the board: *I have went there.* → *I have gone there.*
- Learners work alone and quietly. Call pairs for the speaking interview (4 minutes per pair).
:::

:::step Step 4 · 6:45–7:00 · Break · 15 min
- Collect nothing yet: learners turn the test over. Continue interviews if needed.
:::

:::step Step 5 · 7:00–7:12 · D. Reading · 12 min
- Read the instruction. Learners read the text and answer. Continue interviews.
:::

:::step Step 6 · 7:12–7:27 · E. Writing · 15 min
- Read both tasks aloud. Learners check the box for the task they chose and write 5–8 sentences.
- Say: "Give reasons. Read the questions under your task." At 7:25, say: "Two more minutes. Read your writing one time."
:::

:::step Step 7 · 7:27–7:30 · Self-check and goodbye · 3 min
- Collect the tests. Hand out the Can-Do Self-Check. Learners check the boxes and write one goal. Collect it.
- Finish any last interviews after class or at the start of the next class.
:::

### Clock-time plan

| Time | Whole class | Teacher |
|---|---|---|
| 6:00–6:10 | Warm-up questions in pairs | Interview pair 1 |
| 6:10–6:25 | A. Listening | Play the audio two times |
| 6:25–6:45 | B. Words, C. Grammar | Interviews |
| 6:45–7:00 | Break | Interviews if needed |
| 7:00–7:12 | D. Reading | Interviews |
| 7:12–7:27 | E. Writing | Interviews |
| 7:27–7:30 | Self-check | Collect everything |

:::box Speaking interview (4 minutes per pair)
- **Part 1 (about 2 minutes).** Ask each learner 2 of the questions on the Speaking Interview Card. After each answer, ask one follow-up question (Why? When? Who with? What happened next?).
- **Part 2 (about 2 minutes).** Give the pair one Task Card. They read it for 20 seconds and then talk to each other. You listen and only help if they stop.
- **Score** each learner on the 3 rubric areas (1–3 each, 3–9 in total). Write the score at once, before the next pair.
:::

### Speaking rubric (1–3 in each area)

| Area | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Communication | Short answers only; needs a lot of help. | Answers in 1–2 sentences, or gives no reason, or does not respond to the partner. | Answers in about 3 connected sentences with a reason **and** responds to the partner (a follow-up question, or polite agreement or disagreement). |
| Target grammar | Few lesson forms; many mistakes stop the meaning. | Uses some lesson forms (have + past participle, would, might, to + verb); mistakes do not stop the meaning. | Uses lesson forms correctly most of the time. |
| Pronunciation and fluency | Hard to understand; long stops. | Understandable with some effort; some stops. | Easy to understand; few stops. |

*From Level 4 Scope §7 (the Level 3 rubric, with a higher bar for Communication). Score 3–9 in total.*

## Listening Script

### Listening script: `L4_Midterm_listening.mp3` (about 1:57, estimated from the script; not recorded yet; play it two times)

Speakers: Amina, Ms. Park, Omar, and a narrator.

> **Narrator:** Midterm listening. Part one. Amina is at the Riverside Health Clinic. She is talking to the nurse, Ms. Park. Listen.
> **Ms. Park:** Hi, Amina. What brings you in today?
> **Amina:** I've had a rash on my arm, and it hurts a little.
> **Ms. Park:** I'm sorry. How long have you had it?
> **Amina:** I've had it for about two weeks. It started after I changed my laundry soap.
> **Ms. Park:** Have you ever had a rash like this before?
> **Amina:** No, never. But I've had allergies since I was a child.
> **Ms. Park:** Are you taking any medicine for it?
> **Amina:** Yes. I've already taken some allergy pills, but they haven't helped yet.
> **Ms. Park:** OK. The doctor is going to look at it in a few minutes. Why don't you stop using the new soap? And it's a good idea to bring it next time.
> **Amina:** Sure. I'll do that. Thank you.
> **Narrator:** Part two. That evening, Amina knocks on her neighbor Omar's door.
> **Omar:** Hi, Amina! How was the clinic?
> **Amina:** Fine, thanks. Omar, can I ask you a favor?
> **Omar:** Sure. What is it?
> **Amina:** I'm going to be away this weekend. Would you mind feeding my cat?
> **Omar:** Not at all. I'd be happy to. Do you want me to get the mail, too?
> **Amina:** That would be great. Here's my spare key.
> **Omar:** Do you mind if I bring my son? He loves cats.
> **Amina:** Of course not. And would you mind watering the plants? One time is enough.
> **Omar:** No problem. When are you coming back?
> **Amina:** On Sunday night. Thank you so much. I owe you one!
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

## Scoring Guide

### Written test: score bands (proposed)

| Written score | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 39–50 | **Meets** | The learner can use most of the Lesson 1–7 language on their own. |
| 26–38 | **Almost** | The learner understands a lot but still needs practice. Look at which part was low. |
| 0–25 | **Not yet** | Talk with the learner. Plan extra practice before the final. |

*These bands are **proposed** (about 78% and 52%, the same as the Level 1 and Level 2 exams). The program confirms them after the first classes use the test.*

### Writing rubric (Part E, 6 points)

| Area | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task | Off topic or fewer than 4 sentences. | On topic; 4 sentences, or a part of the task is missing. | Does the task in 5–8 sentences, with reasons. |
| Grammar | Many mistakes; the meaning is hard to follow. | Some mistakes in the lesson forms. | Uses the lesson forms for the task with few mistakes. |
| Clear for a reader | A reader cannot follow it. | A reader understands most of it after reading it two times. | A reader understands it on the first reading. |

*Spelling mistakes do not lose points if the word is clear. Score what the learner wrote, not what is missing from the model answer.*

### Speaking (3–9, separate from the written score)

- 7–9: strong for this level. 5–6: on track. 3–4: needs more speaking practice. *(Proposed.)*

### After the midterm

- Give back the tests in the next class. Go over Part C together: learners correct their own mistakes.
- For learners in the **Not yet** band, choose 2 lessons to review with them (the lesson numbers are in the table above).
- The final exam is in Week 15 and covers all 13 lessons, with more items from Lessons 8–13.

## Teacher Notes

- Midterm: the listening has two parts (Amina at a clinic; Amina and her neighbor). Read the scene line aloud before you play it.
- Part D4: 1:00, 1, one, and 1 p.m. are all correct.
- Part C2: accept any correct sentence that fixes the one mistake; some other good answers are in the key.
- All test items use only words and grammar taught in the Level 4 lessons. If a learner missed a lesson, look at the lesson number in the answer key before you decide what to review.
- Learners may ask what a word in an instruction means. You may explain the instruction, but not the words that are tested.
- Learners who need more time can finish Part E at the start of the next class.

## Papers to Copy

### Speaking Interview Card (Teacher)

**Part 1. Ask each learner 2 questions. Then ask one follow-up question.**

1. Have you ever tried a new food? Tell me about it.
2. How long have you lived in this city?
3. Tell me about a job or a home you had before.
4. What have you already done this week? What haven't you done yet?
5. What would you do if you had a free day tomorrow?
6. Is it OK to be late to a party here? What about in your home country?

**Follow-up questions:** Why? When was that? Who were you with? What happened next? How did you feel?

**Part 2. Give the pair one Task Card.** Listen for:

- **Card A (A clinic check-in):** How long have you had…? for / since, ever / never, already / yet, Why don't you… / It's a good idea to…; about 3 sentences per answer.
- **Card B (What would you do?):** If I found…, I'd…, If I were you…, a reason, I agree / I see your point, but….

### Speaking Task Cards

*Cut out the cards. Give one card to each pair.*

**Learner A** is a patient. Choose a symptom (a rash, a cough, a headache, feeling dizzy).
**Learner B** is a nurse. Ask: How long have you had it? Have you ever had it before? Have you taken any medicine? Then give one suggestion. Change roles.

:::box Card B: What would you do?
You find an envelope with $200 in it on the bus. There is no name on it.
Each learner says what he or she would do, and why. Then agree or disagree with the partner politely.
:::
