### Listening script

Audio is recorded (about 1:43). Read it with volunteers at a natural, friendly speed. The spoken lines use *going to* and *want to*. *gonna* and *wanna* are short forms. This listening uses the full forms.

> **Narrator:** Lesson five. Diego calls Northline Phone about a practice statement. Nothing on this call is a real bill.
> **Diego:** Hello. I want to understand my statement. A fee was added, and I am going to ask about it.
> **Ms. Brooks:** I can help. The fee was added on Tuesday. The fee was $6.
> **Diego:** Was the payment received?
> **Ms. Brooks:** Yes, it was. The payment was received on Friday. The payment was forty dollars.
> **Diego:** And the credit? I want to know what that word means.
> **Ms. Brooks:** The credit was posted by the phone company. Credit means money back. It is not a new phone.
> **Diego:** Was the fee removed?
> **Ms. Brooks:** No, it wasn't. The six dollars was credited. That is the money back.
> **Diego:** Are the calls listed?
> **Ms. Brooks:** Yes, they are. They are listed on page 2. The bill is mailed on the first of the month.
> **Diego:** What is due today?
> **Ms. Brooks:** Nothing is due today. Due today is $0. The due date on the statement is the 20th.
> **Diego:** Thank you. I am going to read the due date again at home. The balance is $28.
> **Ms. Brooks:** That is right. For a real charge, call the number on your own statement. This call is practice.
> **Narrator:** Diego writes two lines: the fee was added on Tuesday, and the payment was received on Friday.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Check the meaning:* 1 yes, 2 a receipt, 3 credit.
- *Listening Task A:* **b** (A fee was added, and a payment was posted.)
- *Listening Task B:* 1 on Tuesday, 2 on Friday, 3 $6, 4 money back, 5 $0.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 was added, 2 was received, 3 was posted, 4 is mailed, 5 are listed, 6 wasn't removed.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 added, 2 received, 3 posted, 4 mailed, 5 listed, 6 high.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 was added, 2 was received, 3 posted the payment, 4 by the phone company, 5 Yes, they are., 6 No, it wasn't..
- *Read & Write B:* 1 On Tuesday., 2 On Friday., 3 $6., 4 Money back., 5 $0..
- *Exit ticket:* answers will vary. A good answer uses tonight's grammar.

### Teacher notes

- **This lesson is not financial advice. Credit means money back on a statement. Do not recommend a loan, a card, or a bank.**
- **Never ask what learners earn, owe, or spend. All amounts are on the page. If a real charge looks wrong, call the number on your own statement.**
- **Reductions:** the listening says *going to* and *want to*. People often say *gonna* and *wanna*. Learners only need to recognize the short forms.
- **All names, places, and numbers are invented.**

## Lesson 5 (Week 5) · Grammar

**Read across. A reads the question. B says the answer.**

### Grammar Box 1: is, are, was, were

| SENTENCE | FORM |
|---|---|
| The bill **is mailed** on the first. | present passive |
| The fee **was added** on Tuesday. | past passive |
| The payment **was received** on Friday. | past passive |
### Grammar Box 2: add by when you need the name

| SENTENCE | WHY |
|---|---|
| The credit **was posted by** the phone company. | by names the doer |
| The fee **was added by** the company. | use by when you need the name |
| The payment **was received**. | no by: start with the thing |
### Grammar Box 3: yes and no

| QUESTION | ANSWER |
|---|---|
| **Are** the calls **listed**? | Yes, they are. |
| **Was** the fee **removed**? | No, it wasn't. |
| **Was** the payment **received**? | Yes, it was. |
### Grammar Box 4: start with the thing

| SAY THIS | NOT THIS IDEA |
|---|---|
| The fee **is too high**. | one review line |
| The payment **was posted**. | start with the thing |
| The company **posted** the payment. | active, when the person matters |

**Rule of thumb:** When the paper cares about the thing, start with the thing. The verb is a form of be plus the past participle. Add by only when you need the name.

### Practice A: Write the missing word.

1. The fee was ____________ on Tuesday.
2. The payment was ____________ on Friday.
3. The credit was ____________ by the phone company.
4. The bill is ____________ on the first.
5. The calls are ____________.
6. The fee is too ____________.

### Practice B: Circle the correct words.

1. The fee (**was added** / **was add**)
2. The payment (**was received** / **received**)
3. The company (**posted the payment** / **was posted the payment**)
4. The credit was posted (**by the phone company** / **for the phone company**)
5. Are the calls listed? (**Yes, they are.** / **Yes, they listed.**)
6. Was the fee removed? (**No, it wasn't.** / **No, it didn't.**)

### Common mistakes

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| The fee was add on Tuesday. | The fee was added on Tuesday. |
| The company was posted the payment. | The payment was posted. |
| The credit was posted for the company. | The credit was posted by the company. |

## Lesson 5 (Week 5) · Read & Write

### A. Read. *Read once. Then find the answers.*

:::box Northline Phone · practice statement
This page is a practice phone statement. It is not a real bill. It is not financial advice. Do not use your own account number, and do not tell the class what you earn or what you owe. Every amount below is invented.

Northline Phone mailed the bill on the first of the month. The bill is mailed on the first, every month. On this statement, a fee was added on Tuesday. The fee was $6. The payment was received on Friday. The payment was $40. A credit was posted by the phone company. Credit on this page means money back. It is not a loan, and it is not a new phone.

The credit was $6, the same amount as the fee. The balance on the statement is $28. The due date is the 20th. Nothing is due today. Due today is $0. The calls are listed on the second page of the statement. The fee was not removed. It was credited, which means the money came back as a line on the statement.

Read the lines in this order. First find the fee. Then find the payment. Then find the credit. Then find the due date. If you need the name of the company, add by: the credit was posted by the phone company. If the paper cares about the payment, and not about a person, start with the payment: the payment was received.

Here is a three-line budget with invented numbers. Money in: $800. Bills: $500. Left: $300. You can say two passive sentences about it. The rent was paid. The fee was not included in the rent. You can also say, the fee is too high, if $6 feels high for this practice bill. Too high is a review from an earlier level. It is one line, not the main point.

If a charge looks wrong on a real statement, call the number on your own statement. Do not practice that call with a real account number. Tonight, Diego calls a practice desk. Ms. Brooks tells him the fee was added on Tuesday and the payment was received on Friday. She tells him that nothing is due today.

When you write, use six sentences and a three-line budget. Two of the sentences should be passive. Use invented numbers only. Name the fee, the payment, and the credit. Say what credit means on this page: money back.
:::
