### Listening script

Audio is not recorded yet (about 2:25). Read it with volunteers at a natural, friendly speed. The spoken lines use *going to* and *want to*. *gonna* and *wanna* are short forms. This listening uses the full forms.

> **Narrator:** Lesson six. Jana is in the waiting room. She calls the clinic. This checkup has no symptoms. It is only a time change.
> **Jana:** Hello. I want to check my appointment. I am going to write down every detail.
> **Nurse Patel:** The checkup is on Thursday. The time has changed.
> **Jana:** Thursday. I want to be sure I have it.
> **Nurse Patel:** Please call before three o'clock. And please bring the appointment card.
> **Jana:** I am going to call before three o'clock, and I am going to bring the card.
> **Nurse Patel:** We will call you if the room changes. Is Thursday OK?
> **Jana:** Yes. Thursday is OK.
> **Narrator:** The call ends. Jana is home now. She tells her brother Ken. Ken was not on the clinic call.
> **Jana:** The nurse said the checkup was on Thursday. She told me the time had changed.
> **Ken:** What did she ask you to do?
> **Jana:** She asked me to call before three o'clock. She asked me to bring the appointment card.
> **Ken:** Did she ask a yes or no question?
> **Jana:** She asked if Thursday was OK. I said yes.
> **Ken:** Anything else?
> **Jana:** She said they would call me if the room changes. I am going to put the note in the folder.
> **Ken:** I can read it back. Thursday, call before three o'clock, bring the card.
> **Narrator:** The message has who, the day, and what to do. That is enough.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Check the meaning:* 1 a note, 2 the waiting room, 3 a family member.
- *Listening Task A:* **b** (No. It moved to Thursday.)
- *Listening Task B:* 1 Thursday, 2 3:00, 3 the appointment card, 4 Ken, 5 the room changes.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 said, 2 told, 3 asked, 4 asked, 5 said, 6 asked.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 said, 2 told, 3 asked, 4 asked, 5 would, 6 me.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 said the day had changed, 2 told me the new time, 3 asked me to call, 4 if Thursday was OK, 5 would call, 6 could come.
- *Read & Write B:* 1 Thursday., 2 Call before 3:00., 3 The appointment card., 4 They would call if the room changes., 5 Ken, a family member..
- *Exit ticket:* answers will vary. A good answer uses tonight's grammar.

### Teacher notes

- **This lesson is not medical advice. For a real health question, ask your clinic. In an emergency, call 911.**
- **Do not ask about real symptoms, medicines, or family health. The nurse scene ends before the home scene starts.**
- **Reductions:** the listening says *going to* and *want to*. People often say *gonna* and *wanna*. Learners only need to recognize the short forms.
- **All names, places, and numbers are invented.**

## Lesson 6 (Week 6) · Listening

*Jana calls the clinic. Nurse Patel moves a checkup to Thursday. The call ends. At home, Jana tells her brother Ken what was said.*

### A. Listen. Did the checkup stay on the same day? Circle.

**a.** Yes. It stayed on the same day.     **b.** No. It moved to Thursday.     **c.** It was canceled.

### B. Listen again. Complete the message.

1. New day (**Thursday** / **Monday** / **Friday**)
2. Call before (**3:00** / **5:00** / **9:00**)
3. Bring (**the appointment card** / **a lunch** / **a photo**)
4. She tells the message to (**Ken** / **the nurse** / **a teacher**)
5. They will call if (**the room changes** / **the clinic closes** / **the class is off**)

### C. Listen again. Write said, told, or asked.

1. She ____________ the checkup was on Thursday.
2. She ____________ me the time had changed.
3. She ____________ me to call before 3:00.
4. She ____________ if Thursday was OK.
5. She ____________ they would call me.
6. She ____________ me to bring the card.

### Hear it, say it

*gonna* and *wanna* are short forms. This listening uses the full forms. You can say *going to* and *want to*.

Said is one syllable. Link told me. If you say that, keep it weak. Say each line two times.

1. I want to write the message down.
2. I am going to call before 3:00.
3. She told me to bring the card.
4. She said they would call.

## Lesson 6 (Week 6) · Grammar

**Read across. A reads the question. B says the answer.**

### Grammar Box 1: said

| DIRECT | REPORTED |
|---|---|
| The nurse said, "The checkup is on Thursday." | She **said** the checkup **was** on Thursday. |
| "We will call you." | She **said** they **would** call me. |
| She said the time had changed. | said does not need a person |
### Grammar Box 2: told

| SAY THIS | NOTE |
|---|---|
| She **told me** the time had changed. | tell needs a person |
| She told me the checkup was on Thursday. | told me, not told to me |
| She told Ken the new day. | a person after told |
### Grammar Box 3: asked me to

| REQUEST | FORM |
|---|---|
| She **asked me to** call before 3:00. | a request |
| She **asked me to** bring the card. | asked me to + verb |
| She asked me to write it in the folder. | a request |
### Grammar Box 4: asked if

| QUESTION | REPORTED |
|---|---|
| She **asked if** Thursday was OK. | statement order |
| She asked if I **could** come before 3:00. | can becomes could |
| She asked if Ken was at home. | yes or no, then statement order |

**Rule of thumb:** Told me, not told to me, and not said me. For a request, asked me to plus a verb. For a yes or no question, asked if plus statement order.

### Practice A: Write said, told, asked, would, or me.

1. She ____________ the checkup was on Thursday.
2. She ____________ me the time had changed.
3. She ____________ me to call before 3:00.
4. She ____________ if Thursday was OK.
5. She said they ____________ call me.
6. She told ____________ the new day.

### Practice B: Circle the correct words.

1. She (**said the day had changed** / **said me the day had changed**)
2. She (**told me the new time** / **told to me the new time**)
3. She (**asked me to call** / **asked that I call**)
4. She asked (**if Thursday was OK** / **if was Thursday OK**)
5. She said they (**would call** / **will call**)
6. She asked if I (**could come** / **can come**)

### Common mistakes

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| She said me the day. | She told me the day. |
| She told that the time had changed. | She said that the time had changed. |
| She asked me if I can come. | She asked if I could come. |
| She told to me. | She told me. |

## Lesson 6 (Week 6) · Read & Write

### A. Read. *Read once. Then find the answers.*

:::box Phone log · Monday 2:10
This page is a practice phone log. It is not medical advice. It does not name a symptom, a medicine, or a test result. The only news is a new day and time for a checkup. For a real health question, ask your clinic. In an emergency, call 911.

Phone log. Monday, 2:10. Nurse Patel called Jana, and Jana called back. The nurse said, "The checkup is on Thursday." She told Jana that the time had changed. She asked Jana to call before 3:00. She asked Jana to bring the appointment card. She asked if Thursday was OK. Jana said yes.

The nurse also said, "We will call you if the room changes." When Jana reports that sentence later, will becomes would. She said they would call her if the room changes. Say does not need a person. Tell does. Jana can say, "She told me the time had changed." She should not say, "She said me," and she should not say, "She told to me."

The call ends before Jana goes home. At home, she tells her brother Ken. Ken was not on the clinic call. Jana is the person who passes the message on. A message needs three details so another person can act: who called, what day, and what to do.

Here is the message Jana would leave. "The nurse said the checkup was on Thursday. She told me the time had changed. She asked me to call before 3:00. She asked me to bring the appointment card. She asked if Thursday was OK. She said they would call me if the room changes."

Ken reads it back. He can say who, what day, and what to do. Who: Nurse Patel. Day: Thursday. What to do: call before 3:00 and bring the appointment card. If one of those is missing, the message is not enough. Add the missing line. Do not add a symptom. There is no symptom in this log.

Write the message in six to eight sentences. Use said or told, asked me to, and asked if. The quote can be the practice message. It should not be a classmate's private life. Keep the note in the folder. Write the callback number only if it is the invented number on this page.
:::
