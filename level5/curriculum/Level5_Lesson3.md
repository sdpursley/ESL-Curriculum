### Listening script

Audio is not recorded yet (about 2:10). Read it with volunteers at a natural, friendly speed. The spoken lines use *going to* and *want to*. *gonna* and *wanna* are short forms. This listening uses the full forms.

> **Narrator:** Lesson three. After class, Alicia and Chris talk about a workshop. They signed up late.
> **Alicia:** I want to take the workshop, but the sign-up sheet was already full.
> **Chris:** I am going to be honest. I wish I had set an alarm.
> **Alicia:** I wish I knew the deadline sooner. I wish I had asked the tutor on Monday.
> **Chris:** If you had asked, you would have known.
> **Alicia:** Yes. If I had asked, I would have known. I should have asked earlier.
> **Chris:** I wish I had put a reminder on my calendar. The alarm on my phone was off.
> **Alicia:** Next time I will ask on the first day.
> **Chris:** I am going to put the reminder on the calendar tonight.
> **Alicia:** If I had another chance, I would ask on the first day. I would not wait.
> **Chris:** If I had set the alarm, I would have arrived on time to sign up.
> **Alicia:** We can still practice. I want to bring one question next week.
> **Chris:** Good. Let us ask at the help desk if we are not sure which choice to make.
> **Alicia:** I am going to write the next step before I leave.
> **Narrator:** They choose a next step. The regret stays small. It is only about the sign-up.
> **Narrator:** That's the end of the listening.

### Answer key

- *Check the meaning:* 1 yes, 2 an alarm, 3 the help desk.
- *Listening Task A:* **b** (They signed up for the workshop late.)
- *Listening Task B:* 1 asked the tutor, 2 set an alarm, 3 ask on the first day, 4 would have known.
- *Listening Task C:* 1 wish I had, 2 would have, 3 wish I had, 4 would have, 5 would have, 6 wish I had.
- *Grammar Practice A:* 1 knew, 2 had, 3 would, 4 should, 5 will, 6 had.
- *Grammar Practice B:* 1 knew, 2 had asked, 3 had asked, 4 should have asked, 5 will ask, 6 would ask.
- *Read & Write B:* 1 The workshop sign-up., 2 I wish I knew the deadline., 3 I wish I had asked the tutor., 4 If I had asked, I would have known., 5 Ask on the first day..
- *Exit ticket:* answers will vary. A good answer uses tonight's grammar.

### Teacher notes

- **Cards stay on class, work schedules, and sign-ups. Do not grade the feeling. No cards about family loss, money trouble, or leaving a country.**
- **Any next step with a reason is a good answer.**
- **Reductions:** the listening says *going to* and *want to*. People often say *gonna* and *wanna*. Learners only need to recognize the short forms.
- **All names, places, and numbers are invented.**

## Lesson 3 (Week 3) · Listening

*After class, Alicia and Chris talk about a workshop they signed up for late. They wish they had asked sooner.*

### A. Listen. What do they regret? Circle.

**a.** They lost a book.     **b.** They signed up for the workshop late.     **c.** They want to leave the class.

### B. Listen again. Circle the answers.

1. Alicia's wish (**asked the tutor** / **left the class** / **skipped practice**)
2. Chris's wish (**set an alarm** / **changed jobs** / **missed the class**)
3. Next time Alicia will (**ask on the first day** / **wait a year** / **stay quiet**)
4. The if sentence uses (**would have known** / **will know** / **knew**)

### C. Listen again. Write wish I had or would have.

1. Alicia: "I ____________ asked the tutor."
2. "If I had asked, I ____________ known the date."
3. Chris: "I ____________ set an alarm."
4. "If I had set one, I ____________ arrived on time."
5. Alicia: "Then I ____________ known."
6. Chris: "I ____________ put a reminder on the calendar."

### Hear it, say it

*gonna* and *wanna* are short forms. This listening uses the full forms. You can say *going to* and *want to*.

In I wish I had asked, say the full had. In I would ask next time, say the full would. Say each line two times.

1. I want to ask the tutor.
2. I am going to sign up on the first day.
3. I wish I had asked.
4. If I had asked, I would have known.

## Lesson 3 (Week 3) · Grammar

**Read across. A reads the question. B says the answer.**

### Grammar Box 1: a wish about now

| WISH | MEANING |
|---|---|
| I wish I **knew** the deadline. | a wish about now |
| I wish I **had** a reminder. | had means owned |
| I wish I **were** on the list. | were, for now |
### Grammar Box 2: a wish about the past

| WISH | MEANING |
|---|---|
| I wish I **had asked** the tutor. | a past regret |
| I wish I **had set** an alarm. | had + participle |
| I wish we **had signed** up on Monday. | the sign-up is finished |
### Grammar Box 3: one different past

| IF | RESULT |
|---|---|
| If I **had asked**, I **would have known**. | one pattern |
| If he **had set** an alarm, he **would have arrived** on time. | same pattern |
| If we **had read** the reminder, we **would have signed** up. | same pattern |
### Grammar Box 4: should have, and next time

| NOW | NEXT |
|---|---|
| I **should have asked** earlier. | same idea as the wish |
| Next time I **will ask** on the first day. | a plan |
| If I **had** another chance, I **would ask**. | next time |

**Rule of thumb:** A wish about now uses a past verb (knew, had, were). A wish about the past uses had + participle. After If I had + participle, the other half is would have + participle.

### Practice A: Write the missing word.

1. I wish I ____________ the deadline.
2. I wish I ____________ asked the tutor.
3. If I had asked, I ____________ have known.
4. I ____________ have asked earlier.
5. Next time I ____________ ask on the first day.
6. I wish I ____________ a reminder.

### Practice B: Circle the correct words.

1. I wish I (**knew** / **know**)
2. I wish I (**had asked** / **would have asked**)
3. If I (**had asked** / **would have asked**)
4. I (**should have asked** / **should asked**)
5. Next time I (**will ask** / **would have ask**)
6. If I had another chance, I (**would ask** / **would have asked**)

### Common mistakes

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| I wish I would have asked. | I wish I had asked. |
| If I would have asked, I would have known. | If I had asked, I would have known. |
| I wish I know the deadline. | I wish I knew the deadline. |
| I should asked earlier. | I should have asked earlier. |

## Lesson 3 (Week 3) · Read & Write

### A. Read. *Read once. Then find the answers.*

:::box A note from the tutor
You missed the workshop sign-up. This note is from the class tutor. It is practice for class. It is not a grade. It is not about your family, your money, or a job. The problem is small. The sign-up date passed before you wrote your name on the sheet.

Start with a wish about now. You can say, "I wish I knew the deadline." Knew is a past form, and it talks about right now. You do not know the deadline today. You can also say, "I wish I had a reminder on my calendar." In that sentence, had means owned. It is not the regret form. A third line is, "I wish I were on the list." Were is the form we use after wish when we mean now.

Then say a wish about the past. "I wish I had asked the tutor." The asking is finished, and you are sorry you did not do it. Do not say, "I wish I would have asked." After wish, a past regret uses had plus the past participle. Had asked, had set, and had signed are the forms for tonight.

One different past is enough. "If I had asked, I would have known." The if half uses had plus a participle. The other half uses would have plus a participle. You do not need every person, and you do not need both clause orders. One clear sentence is the goal. Chris can say, "If I had set an alarm, I would have arrived on time."

You can put the same idea in a shorter line. "I should have asked earlier." That line is one row in the grammar box. It is not a new lesson. It means the same thing as the wish about the past. Use it once, then move to the next step.

Next time, make a plan you can do. "Next time I will ask on the first day." You can also say, "If I had another chance, I would ask on the first day." Put a reminder on the calendar. Set an alarm the day before. Bring one question to practice. If you are not sure which choice to make, ask at the help desk.
:::
