### D. Reading (8 points)

Read the text. Then answer the questions. Circle the answer or write one word or number.

> **Community Blog · How We Opened Our Bakery · by Marta Silva**
>
> My husband and I have been baking bread for twenty years, but we had never owned a business. Last spring, we finally opened Silva Bakery on Main Street. Here is our story.
>
> First, we rented an old shop. The walls were painted, and a new counter and display case were added. Then we needed a permit. The permit was approved in March, but the inspector couldn't come until April. By the time he arrived, we had already bought two ovens and made the store sign. He checked the kitchen for an hour. Even though he found a small problem with a door, he said that we could open as long as we fixed it in two weeks. We fixed it the next day. Opening day was May 10. Our daughter cut the ribbon, and more than a hundred people came. By noon, we had sold all the bread. Some customers even asked us for a job. It was the best day of our lives.
>
> Of course, we made some mistakes. I wish we had hired a second worker before opening day. We were very tired! If we had asked for help earlier, we would have had more time for our customers. Also, some receipts were lost in the first month, so now we keep them all in one folder. My husband double-checks the money every evening before we close.
>
> Next month, we're going to start a bread class on Saturday mornings. You can join for free, as long as you sign up at the counter. We hope to see you soon!
