>
> Thirty people came to the March meeting, which was held in Room 4, where the English classes meet. Grace Park was the chairperson, and Leo Garcia took the minutes. Here is a short report.
>
> The first item on the agenda was the parking lot. Some neighbors had seen a post online that said the lot was going to close. Ms. Park said that she had called the city office to ask whether it was true. It isn't. The post had no author and no date stamp. It can't be an official notice. Ms. Park asked everyone to check the source before they share a post.
>

After a long talk, the groups found a compromise. The art class will use the room from 9:00 to 11:00, and the walking club will meet there after 11:00. Everyone agreed that this was fair.
>
